# Nonresident Forms and Deadlines

## Core annual forms for J-1 work

| Form | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `Form 8843` | Statement supporting exempt-individual treatment for substantial-presence purposes |
| `Form 1040-NR` | Annual income tax return for a nonresident alien with reportable U.S. income |
| `Form 8233` | Treaty-based withholding exemption or reduction for compensation for personal services |
| `Form 8833` | Disclosure of certain treaty-based return positions, including common tie-breaker situations |

## Tax Year 2025 filing dates

- `Form 1040-NR`: due `April 15, 2026` if the taxpayer had wages subject to U.S. withholding.
- `Form 1040-NR`: due `June 15, 2026` if the taxpayer did not have wages subject to U.S. withholding.
- `Form 8843` filed by itself uses the same due date rule as `Form 1040-NR`.

## Filing note for `Form 8843`

- If a 2025 `Form 1040-NR` is filed, attach `Form 8843` to that return.
- If no 2025 return is required, mail `Form 8843` by the applicable `Form 1040-NR` due date to:
  `Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service Center, Austin, TX 73301-0215`

## Documents to request early

- Passport identity page and visa class
- `DS-2019`
- U.S. entry dates for all years relevant to the substantial presence test
- `SSN` or `ITIN` status
- `W-2`
- `1042-S`
- `1099` forms
- Prior-year U.S. tax returns and prior-year `8843` filings
- Treaty article and country of tax residence if a treaty benefit is claimed

## Quick screen

- No taxable income and still nonresident: usually `8843` only.
- Nonresident with taxable income: usually `1040-NR` plus `8843`.
- Resident under substantial presence test: usually resident-return path unless a treaty tie-breaker changes the result.
