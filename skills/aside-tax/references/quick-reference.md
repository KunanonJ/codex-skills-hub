# 2025 Quick Reference

Selected 2025 figures for orientation. Confirm details in the cited instruction files before relying on them.

## Caution for nonresident filers

These figures are mainly useful in resident-style `Form 1040` workflows. A nonresident alien filing `Form 1040-NR` often cannot use the standard deduction or many resident-only credits. Start with `references/nonresident-aliens/overview.md` before applying the tables below to J-1 or other visa-based filing questions.
