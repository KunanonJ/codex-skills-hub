# Foreign Tax Credit

If a return shows foreign taxes paid, start by checking whether the taxpayer fits the simplified foreign tax credit rule.

## Simplified rule

The shortcut lets a taxpayer claim the credit directly on Schedule 3 without filing Form 1116 if all of the usual conditions are met, including:

1. The foreign-source income was passive-category interest or dividends.
2. The foreign taxes were reported on standard information returns such as Forms 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, or Schedule K-3.
3. Total foreign taxes were not more than $300 for single, HOH, or QSS, or $600 for MFJ.
4. The holding-period and no-pass-through-obligation rules are satisfied.
5. The taxpayer is not in one of the listed Form 1116 exception categories.

## Practical note

For ordinary brokerage accounts, the dollar threshold is often the first thing to check.

## When Form 1116 becomes relevant

If the shortcut does not apply, read the Schedule 3 foreign tax credit instructions together with the IRS Form 1116 instructions. The local skill materials do not package Form 1116 instructions.
