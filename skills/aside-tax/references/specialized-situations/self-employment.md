# Self-Employment

Self-employment issues often span Schedule C, Schedule SE, Schedule 1, Schedule 2, Form 1040, and sometimes Form 8995 or 8995-A.

## Key instruction files

- `irs-instructions/f1040sc/` for Schedule C income, expenses, and business-use questions
- `irs-instructions/f1040sse/` for self-employment tax and net-earnings rules
- `irs-instructions/f8995/` and `irs-instructions/f8995a/` when QBI is in play

## Cross-form points

- Schedule C net profit or loss feeds Schedule 1.
- Schedule SE determines self-employment tax and the deductible half that flows back to Schedule 1.
- Self-employment tax ultimately reaches Schedule 2 and then Form 1040 total tax.
- The deductible half of self-employment tax can also reduce QBI.

## Common watchpoints

- Schedule SE generally matters once net earnings reach $400.
- Only the Social Security part of self-employment tax is capped by the wage base; the Medicare part continues above that amount.
- Joint returns with self-employment income for both spouses usually require separate Schedule SE treatment for each spouse.
