# QBI Deduction (Section 199A)

## Thresholds

| Taxable income before the QBI deduction | Primary form |
|---|---|
| Single at or below $197,300 | Form 8995 |
| MFJ at or below $394,600 | Form 8995 |
| Above those thresholds | Form 8995-A |

## Common points

- QBI often starts with Schedule C or K-1 business income, but it is not always the same as book profit.
- The deductible half of self-employment tax and certain self-employed deductions can reduce QBI.
- Qualified REIT dividends and publicly traded partnership income can also feed the deduction.
- SSTB limits matter only once taxable income exceeds the threshold.

## SSTB note

Health, law, accounting, consulting, financial services, performing arts, athletics, and reputation-or-skill businesses are classic SSTB categories. Engineering and architecture are excluded from the SSTB definition.
