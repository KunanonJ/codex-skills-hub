# AMT Screening

AMT deserves closer attention when the return includes preference items or adjustments that differ from regular tax treatment.

## Common AMT triggers

- ISO exercises
- Private activity bond interest
- Large Schedule A state and local tax deductions
- Accelerated depreciation differences
- Property dispositions with different AMT and regular bases

## 2025 exemption amounts

| Filing status | Exemption | Phaseout begins |
|---|---:|---:|
| Single or head of household | $88,100 | $626,350 |
| Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse | $137,000 | $1,252,700 |
| Married filing separately | $68,500 | $626,350 |
