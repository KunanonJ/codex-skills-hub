# Prior-Year Carryforwards

Many current-year issues depend on facts from earlier returns. Missing carryforward data can materially change the result.

## Common items to look for

| Item | Why it matters | Typical destination |
|---|---|---|
| Capital loss carryforward | Preserves unused short-term or long-term losses | Schedule D |
| Prior-year tax and AGI | Matters for Form 2210 safe-harbor analysis | Form 2210 |
| Nondeductible IRA basis | Prevents double taxation on Form 8606 | Form 8606 |
| Net operating loss carryforward | Can reduce current-year income | Schedule 1 and related worksheets |
| Passive activity loss carryforward | Affects rental and passive-loss treatment | Form 8582 and related schedules |
| AMT credit carryforward | Can offset current-year tax in limited cases | Form 8801 / Schedule 3 |
| Charitable carryforward | Extends excess charitable deductions | Schedule A |

## Notes

- Capital loss carryforwards keep their character as short-term or long-term.
- Prior-year Form 8606 data is especially important for Roth conversions and traditional IRA distributions.
- If the exact amount is unavailable, prior-year return copies, software carryforward worksheets, or IRS transcripts may be needed.
