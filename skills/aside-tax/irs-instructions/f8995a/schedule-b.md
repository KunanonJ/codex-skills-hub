# Form 8995-A Schedule B: Aggregation of Business Operations

## Purpose

Schedule B documents the election to aggregate multiple qualified trades or businesses for purposes of the QBI deduction. Aggregation combines the QBI, W-2 wages, and UBIA of multiple businesses into a single amount before applying the W-2 wage/UBIA limitation.

## When Aggregation is Beneficial

Aggregation helps when one business has high QBI but low W-2 wages, and another has significant W-2 wages. By combining them, the high-wage business effectively "shares" its wages with the low-wage business, potentially increasing the overall QBI deduction.

## Requirements for Aggregation

All of the following must be met:

1. **Common ownership:** The same person or group of persons, directly or indirectly, owns 50% or more of each trade or business
2. **Ownership duration:** The common ownership exists for a majority of the tax year (including the last day)
3. **Same tax year:** All items attributable to each business are reported on returns with the same tax year-end
4. **No SSTBs:** None of the businesses to be aggregated is an SSTB
5. **Two of three factors:**
   a. The businesses provide products and services that are the same or are customarily offered together
   b. The businesses share facilities or share significant centralized business elements (personnel, accounting, legal, manufacturing, purchasing, HR, IT)
   c. The businesses are operated in coordination with, or reliance upon, one or more of the other businesses in the aggregated group

## Filing Requirements

- Complete Schedule B for each aggregated group
- List all businesses included in the aggregation
- The election, once made, is binding for all future years (unless facts and circumstances change significantly)
- A new business may be added to an existing aggregation if it meets all requirements

## How Aggregation Affects Form 8995-A

When businesses are aggregated:
- Report the aggregation as a single row in Part I (check the aggregation box, column (c))
- Enter the **combined** QBI, W-2 wages, and UBIA for the aggregated group in Part II
- The W-2 wage/UBIA limitation is applied to the combined amounts
