# Form 8995-A: Specific Instructions (Line by Line)

## Part I — Trade, Business, or Aggregation Information

**Line 1:** Enter information for each trade, business, or aggregation for which you have QBI. If you have more than three, attach additional sheets.

- **(a)** Trade, business, or aggregation name
- **(b)** Check if the trade or business is a specified service trade or business (SSTB)
- **(c)** Check if this is an aggregation of multiple businesses (complete Schedule B)
- **(d)** Taxpayer identification number (EIN or SSN)
- **(e)** Check if you are a patron of an agricultural or horticultural cooperative (complete Schedule D — not supported)

## Part II — Determine Your Adjusted Qualified Business Income

Complete columns A, B, and C for each business listed in Part I.

**Line 2.** Enter the QBI from the trade, business, or aggregation. This is the net amount of qualified items of income, gain, deduction, and loss. If loss, enter as a negative number and complete Schedule C.

**Line 3.** Multiply line 2 by 20% (0.20). If your taxable income is $197,300 or less ($394,600 if MFJ), **skip lines 4 through 12** and enter the amount from line 3 on line 13. (Note: if taxable income is at or below the threshold, use Form 8995 instead.)

**Line 4.** Enter the allocable share of W-2 wages from the trade, business, or aggregation.

**Line 5.** Multiply line 4 by 50% (0.50). This is the first W-2 wage limitation formula.

**Line 6.** Multiply line 4 by 25% (0.25).

**Line 7.** Enter the allocable share of the UBIA (unadjusted basis immediately after acquisition) of all qualified property.

**Line 8.** Multiply line 7 by 2.5% (0.025).

**Line 9.** Add lines 6 and 8. This is the alternative W-2 wage/UBIA limitation formula (25% of W-2 wages + 2.5% of UBIA).

**Line 10.** Enter the **greater** of line 5 or line 9. This is the W-2 wage/UBIA limitation.

**Line 11.** W-2 wage and UBIA limitation. Enter the **smaller** of line 3 or line 10.

**Line 12.** Phased-in reduction. If your taxable income is within the phase-in range ($197,301–$247,300 single / $394,601–$494,600 MFJ), complete Part III and enter the amount from line 26. If your taxable income is above the phase-in range, skip line 12.

**Line 13.** QBI deduction before patron reduction. Enter the **greater** of line 11 or line 12. If line 12 is blank (taxable income above phase-in range), enter line 11.

**Line 14.** Patron reduction. Enter the amount from Schedule D (Form 8995-A), line 6, if any. (Not supported — enter 0 unless user provides.)

**Line 15.** Subtract line 14 from line 13. This is the QBI component for this business.

**Line 16.** Total qualified business income component. Add all amounts reported on line 15.

## Part III — Phased-in Reduction

Complete Part III **only** if your taxable income is more than $197,300 but not more than $247,300 ($394,600 but not $494,600 if MFJ) **and** line 10 is less than line 3. Otherwise, skip Part III.

**Line 17.** Enter the amounts from line 3 (20% of QBI for each business).

**Line 18.** Enter the amounts from line 10 (W-2 wage/UBIA limitation for each business).

**Line 19.** Subtract line 18 from line 17 for each business. This is the excess of 20% QBI over the limitation.

**Line 20.** Enter taxable income before QBI deduction. (Same value in each column.)

**Line 21.** Enter the threshold: $197,300 ($394,600 if MFJ).

**Line 22.** Subtract line 21 from line 20. This is the excess over the threshold.

**Line 23.** Enter the phase-in range: $50,000 ($100,000 if MFJ).

**Line 24.** Divide line 22 by line 23. Enter as a decimal (not a percentage). This is the phase-in percentage.

**Line 25.** Multiply line 19 by line 24 for each business. This is the phase-in reduction amount.

**Line 26.** Subtract line 25 from line 17 for each business. Enter this amount on line 12 **and** on line 12 for the corresponding business. This is the QBI deduction after phase-in reduction.

## Part IV — Determine Your Qualified Business Income Deduction

**Line 27.** Enter the amount from line 16 (total QBI component from all businesses).

**Line 28.** Enter qualified REIT dividends and publicly traded partnership (PTP) income or loss. See instructions for what qualifies.

**Line 29.** Enter qualified REIT dividends and PTP (loss) carryforward from prior years. Enter as a negative number.

**Line 30.** Combine lines 28 and 29. If the result is less than zero, enter 0.

**Line 31.** REIT and PTP component. Multiply line 30 by 20% (0.20).

**Line 32.** QBI deduction before the income limitation. Add lines 27 and 31.

**Line 33.** Enter taxable income before QBI deduction.

**Line 34.** Enter your net capital gain, if any, increased by any qualified dividends. See instructions.

**Line 35.** Subtract line 34 from line 33. If zero or less, enter 0.

**Line 36.** Income limitation. Multiply line 35 by 20% (0.20).

**Line 37.** Qualified business income deduction before the domestic production activities deduction (DPAD). Enter the **smaller** of line 32 or line 36.

**Line 38.** DPAD under section 199A(g) allocated from an agricultural or horticultural cooperative. Don't enter more than line 33 minus line 37. (Not supported — enter 0.)

**Line 39.** Total qualified business income deduction. Add lines 37 and 38. **Enter this amount on Form 1040, line 13a.**

**Line 40.** Total REIT dividends and PTP (loss) carryforward. Combine lines 28 and 29. If zero or greater, enter 0. This amount carries forward to next year.
