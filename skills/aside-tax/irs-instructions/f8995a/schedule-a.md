# Form 8995-A Schedule A: Specified Service Trades or Businesses

## Purpose

Schedule A computes the reduction to QBI, W-2 wages, and UBIA for specified service trades or businesses (SSTBs) when the taxpayer's taxable income is within the phase-in range.

## When to Complete

Complete Schedule A if:
1. You have QBI from an SSTB, **and**
2. Your taxable income before QBI deduction is within the phase-in range:
   - Single/HoH/MFS: $197,301 – $247,300
   - MFJ: $394,601 – $494,600

**Do not** complete Schedule A if:
- Taxable income is at or below the threshold (SSTBs get full QBI deduction)
- Taxable income is above the phase-in range (SSTBs get $0 — do not include on Form 8995-A at all)

## Computation

The applicable percentage determines how much of the SSTB's QBI, W-2 wages, and UBIA to include:

1. **Excess** = Taxable income before QBI deduction − Threshold
2. **Phase-in percentage** = Excess ÷ Phase-in range (decimal, capped at 1.0)
3. **Applicable percentage** = 1.0 − Phase-in percentage

Apply the applicable percentage:
- **Includible QBI** = SSTB QBI × Applicable percentage
- **Includible W-2 wages** = SSTB W-2 wages × Applicable percentage
- **Includible UBIA** = SSTB UBIA × Applicable percentage

Enter these reduced amounts on Form 8995-A Part II for the corresponding SSTB business.

## Example

Single filer, taxable income $220,000, SSTB with $100,000 QBI, $40,000 W-2 wages, $50,000 UBIA:

1. Excess = $220,000 − $197,300 = $22,700
2. Phase-in percentage = $22,700 ÷ $50,000 = 0.454
3. Applicable percentage = 1.0 − 0.454 = 0.546

- Includible QBI = $100,000 × 0.546 = $54,600
- Includible W-2 wages = $40,000 × 0.546 = $21,840
- Includible UBIA = $50,000 × 0.546 = $27,300

These reduced amounts are then used in Part II for the W-2 wage/UBIA limitation computation.
