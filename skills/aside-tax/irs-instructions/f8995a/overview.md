# Form 8995-A: Qualified Business Income Deduction — Overview

## Purpose

Form 8995-A computes the Section 199A qualified business income (QBI) deduction for taxpayers whose **taxable income before the QBI deduction exceeds**:
- **$197,300** (Single, Head of Household, Married Filing Separately)
- **$394,600** (Married Filing Jointly)

Taxpayers at or below these thresholds use the simplified Form 8995 instead.

## Who Must File Form 8995-A

File Form 8995-A if **all** of the following apply:
1. You have qualified business income, qualified REIT dividends, or qualified PTP income (loss)
2. Your taxable income before the QBI deduction is **above** the threshold for your filing status
3. You are not a patron of an agricultural or horticultural cooperative (or if you are, you also complete Schedule D)

## What's New for 2025

- The income threshold amounts remain $197,300 / $394,600
- The phase-in range remains $50,000 / $100,000
- No structural changes to the form

## Key Thresholds (2025)

## Form Structure

- **Part I** — Trade, Business, or Aggregation Information (up to 3 businesses; attach additional sheets if more)
- **Part II** — Determine Your Adjusted Qualified Business Income (per-business W-2 wage/UBIA limitations, lines 2-16)
- **Part III** — Phased-in Reduction (only if taxable income is in the phase-in range, lines 17-26)
- **Part IV** — Determine Your Qualified Business Income Deduction (final computation, lines 27-40)

## Schedules

- **Schedule A** — Specified Service Trades or Businesses (SSTB phase-in computation)
- **Schedule B** — Aggregation of Business Operations
- **Schedule C** — Loss Netting and Carryforward
- **Schedule D** — Special Rules for Patrons of Agricultural or Horticultural Cooperatives (not supported)

## Form Flow

Form 8995-A line 39 (total QBI deduction) → Form 1040 line 13a
