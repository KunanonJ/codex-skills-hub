## Instructions for Form 2210 - Underpayment of Estimated Tax by Individuals, Estates, and Trusts

### Future Developments

For the latest information about developments related to Form 2210 and its instructions, such as legislation enacted after they were published, go to IRS.gov/Form2210.

### General Instructions

#### Purpose of Form

Use Form 2210 to determine whether you owe a penalty for underpaying your estimated tax. The penalty may apply if you didn't pay enough estimated tax for the year or you didn't make the payments on time or in the required amount.

The penalty is figured separately for each payment period. You may owe a penalty for an earlier payment period even if you paid enough tax later to make up the underpayment.

#### Who Must File Form 2210

Use the flowchart on page 1 of Form 2210 to determine whether you must file. In most cases, you don't need to file Form 2210 because the IRS will figure the penalty, if any, and send you a bill.

**You must file Form 2210 if:**
- Box B, C, or D in Part II applies.

**You should file Form 2210 if:**
- Box A or E applies (file only page 1).
- You want to figure the penalty yourself using Part III.

#### When To File

File Form 2210 with your income tax return (Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR, or 1041).

#### Estimated Tax Payment Due Dates

| Payment Period | Due Date |
|---|---|
| 1st installment | April 15, 2025 |
| 2nd installment | June 15, 2025 |
| 3rd installment | September 15, 2025 |
| 4th installment | January 15, 2026 |

If a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the payment is due the next business day.

#### No Penalty Situations

You **don't owe a penalty** if any of the following applies:

1. The total tax shown on your 2025 return minus the amount of tax you paid through withholding (including excess social security tax withholding) is **less than $1,000**.

2. You paid through withholding and estimated tax at least **100% of the tax shown on your 2024 return** (110% if your 2024 AGI exceeded $150,000, or $75,000 if married filing separately for 2025). Your 2024 return must have covered a 12-month period.

3. You had **no tax liability for 2024** and you were a U.S. citizen or resident for all of 2024.

#### Safe Harbor Rule

You won't owe the penalty if the total of your withholding and timely estimated tax payments equals at least the **smaller** of:

- **90%** of your 2025 tax, or
- **100%** of the tax shown on your 2024 return (**110%** if your 2024 AGI was more than $150,000, or more than $75,000 if married filing separately for 2025).

#### Prior Year Tax for Line 8

The "tax shown on your 2024 return" is the amount on your 2024 Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 24, minus the total of any amounts shown on:
- Lines 27, 28, and 29
- Schedule 3, lines 9 and 12
- Forms 8828, 4137, 5329 (Parts III through IX only), and 8919

Also subtract from line 24 any:
- Tax on an excess parachute payment
- Excise tax on insider stock compensation of an expatriated corporation
- Uncollected social security and Medicare or RRTA tax on tips or group-term life insurance
- Look-back interest due under section 167(g) or 460(b)

If your 2024 AGI was more than $150,000 ($75,000 if married filing separately for 2025), multiply the result by 110% (1.10).

#### Waiver of Penalty

The IRS may waive all or part of the penalty if:

- You retired (after reaching age 62) or became disabled during 2024 or 2025, and the underpayment was due to reasonable cause and not willful neglect.
- A federally declared disaster, casualty, or other unusual circumstance made it inequitable to impose the penalty.

To request a waiver, check box A (entire penalty) or box B (part of penalty) in Part II and attach a statement explaining the circumstances.

### Line Instructions

#### Part I — Required Annual Payment

**Line 1.** Enter your 2025 tax after credits from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 22.

**Line 2.** Enter other taxes, including:
- Self-employment tax (Schedule 2, line 4 or line 6)
- Additional Medicare Tax (Form 8959)
- Net Investment Income Tax (Form 8960)
- Household employment taxes (Schedule 2, line 9), if applicable
- Other taxes from Schedule 2, Part II

Don't include any estimated tax penalty or interest.

**Line 3.** Enter refundable credits and other payments that reduce your tax, including:
- Earned income credit
- Additional child tax credit
- American opportunity credit (refundable portion)
- Premium tax credit
- Credits from Schedule 3, lines 9 through 13 (except line 11)
- Credit for federal tax on fuels

Enter this amount in parentheses (it reduces your current year tax).

**Line 4.** Current year tax. Combine lines 1, 2, and 3. If less than $1,000, **stop** — you don't owe a penalty. Don't file Form 2210.

**Line 5.** Multiply line 4 by 90% (0.90).

**Line 6.** Enter your withholding taxes from Form 1040, line 25d, plus any credit for excess social security tax withheld (Schedule 3, line 11). **Don't** include estimated tax payments.

**Line 7.** Subtract line 6 from line 4. If less than $1,000, **stop** — you don't owe a penalty. Don't file Form 2210.

**Line 8.** Maximum required annual payment based on prior year's tax. See the "Prior Year Tax for Line 8" section above.

If your 2024 AGI was more than $150,000 ($75,000 if married filing separately for 2025), multiply your 2024 tax (as computed above) by 110% (1.10). Otherwise, enter 100% of your 2024 tax.

**Special rule:** If you didn't file a 2024 return or it didn't cover 12 months, you can't use the prior year tax method. Leave line 8 blank and enter the line 5 amount on line 9.

**Line 9.** Required annual payment. Enter the **smaller** of line 5 or line 8.

#### Part II — Reasons for Filing

Check the applicable box(es). If none apply, don't file Form 2210.

**Box A.** You request a **waiver** of your entire penalty. Check this box and file page 1 of Form 2210, but you aren't required to figure your penalty.

**Box B.** You request a **waiver of part** of your penalty. You must figure your penalty and waiver amount and file Form 2210.

**Box C.** Your income varied during the year and your penalty is reduced or eliminated when figured using the **annualized income installment method**. You must figure the penalty using Schedule AI and file Form 2210.

**Box D.** Your penalty is lower when figured by treating the federal income tax withheld from your income as paid on the **dates it was actually withheld**, instead of in equal amounts on the payment due dates. You must figure your penalty and file Form 2210.

**Box E.** You filed or are filing a joint return for either 2024 or 2025, but not for both years, and line 8 above is smaller than line 5 above. You must file page 1 of Form 2210, but you aren't required to figure your penalty (unless box B, C, or D applies).

#### Part III — Penalty Computation

##### Section A — Figure Your Underpayment

Complete lines 12 through 18 of one column before going to line 12 of the next column.

**Line 10.** Required installments. If box C in Part II applies, enter the amounts from Schedule AI, line 27. Otherwise, enter 25% (0.25) of line 9, Form 2210, in each column.

**Line 11.** Estimated tax paid and tax withheld for each payment period:
- **Column (a):** Amounts paid from January 1 through April 15, 2025
- **Column (b):** Amounts paid from April 16 through June 15, 2025
- **Column (c):** Amounts paid from June 16 through September 15, 2025
- **Column (d):** Amounts paid from September 16, 2025, through January 15, 2026

Withholding is generally treated as paid in equal amounts on each due date (25% per period), unless you checked box D in Part II.

For column (a) only, also enter the amount from line 11 on line 15, column (a).

If line 11 is equal to or more than line 10 for **all** payment periods, stop here; you don't owe a penalty. Don't file Form 2210 unless you checked a box in Part II.

**Line 12.** Enter the amount, if any, from line 18 in the **previous** column. (Column (a) is blank.)

**Line 13.** Add lines 11 and 12.

**Line 14.** Add the amounts on lines 16 and 17 in the **previous** column. (Column (a) is blank.)

**Line 15.** Subtract line 14 from line 13. If zero or less, enter -0-. For column (a) only, enter the amount from line 11.

**Line 16.** If line 15 is zero, subtract line 13 from line 14. Otherwise, enter -0-.

**Line 17.** Underpayment. If line 10 is equal to or more than line 15, subtract line 15 from line 10. Then go to line 12 of the next column. Otherwise, go to line 18.

**Line 18.** Overpayment. If line 15 is more than line 10, subtract line 10 from line 15. Then go to line 12 of the next column.

##### Section B — Figure the Penalty

Use the Worksheet for Form 2210, Part III, Section B in the instructions to compute the penalty. The worksheet applies the applicable federal short-term rate plus 3 percentage points (compounded daily) to each quarterly underpayment for the number of days it remains unpaid.

**2025 Penalty Rate Periods:**

| Period | Dates | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1st quarter rate | April 15 – June 30, 2025 | See IRS Rev. Rul. |
| 2nd quarter rate | July 1 – September 30, 2025 | See IRS Rev. Rul. |
| 3rd quarter rate | October 1 – December 31, 2025 | See IRS Rev. Rul. |
| 4th quarter rate | January 1 – April 15, 2026 | See IRS Rev. Rul. |

The rate is announced quarterly. Check IRS.gov for the current rates.

**Line 19.** Enter the total penalty from line 14 of the Worksheet for Form 2210, Part III, Section B. Include this amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 38.

Don't file Form 2210 unless you checked a box in Part II.

#### Schedule AI — Annualized Income Installment Method

Use Schedule AI if your income varied during the year (box C in Part II). This method may lower your required installments for periods when your income was lower.

##### Schedule AI Part I — Annualized Income Installments

**Annualization Periods:**

| Column | Period | Annualization Factor (line 2) |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | 1/1/25 – 3/31/25 | 4 |
| (b) | 1/1/25 – 5/31/25 | 2.4 |
| (c) | 1/1/25 – 8/31/25 | 1.5 |
| (d) | 1/1/25 – 12/31/25 | 1 |

**Line 1.** Enter your adjusted gross income for each period.

**Line 2.** Annualization amounts: 4, 2.4, 1.5, 1.

**Line 3.** Annualized income. Multiply line 1 by line 2.

**Line 4.** If you itemize, enter itemized deductions for the period shown in each column. All others, enter -0- and skip to line 7.

**Line 5.** Annualization amounts: 4, 2.4, 1.5, 1.

**Line 6.** Multiply line 4 by line 5.

**Line 7.** Enter the amount of your standard deduction from Form 1040 or 1040-SR. (Form 1040-NR filers, enter -0-.)

**Line 8.** Enter the **larger** of line 6 or line 7.

**Line 9.** Deduction for qualified business income.

**Line 10.** Add lines 8 and 9.

**Line 11.** Subtract line 10 from line 3.

**Line 12.** Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR filers, enter -0- in each column.

**Line 13.** Subtract line 12 from line 11. If zero or less, enter -0-.

**Line 14.** Figure your tax on the amount on line 13 using the applicable tax rate schedule or worksheet.

**Line 15.** Self-employment tax from line 36 (complete Part II below).

**Line 16.** Enter other taxes for each payment period including, if applicable, Additional Medicare Tax and/or Net Investment Income Tax.

**Line 17.** Total tax. Add lines 14, 15, and 16.

**Line 18.** For each period, enter the same type of credits as allowed on Form 2210, Part I, lines 1 and 3.

**Line 19.** Subtract line 18 from line 17. If zero or less, enter -0-.

**Line 20.** Applicable percentage: 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%, 90%.

**Line 21.** Multiply line 19 by line 20.

Complete lines 22–27 of one column before going to line 22 of the next column.

**Line 22.** Enter the total of the amounts in all previous columns of line 27.

**Line 23.** Subtract line 22 from line 21. If zero or less, enter -0-.

**Line 24.** Enter 25% (0.25) of line 9 on page 1 of Form 2210 in each column.

**Line 25.** Subtract line 27 of the previous column from line 26 of that column.

**Line 26.** Add lines 24 and 25.

**Line 27.** Enter the **smaller** of line 23 or line 26 here and on Form 2210, Part III, line 10.

##### Schedule AI Part II — Annualized Self-Employment Tax

For Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR filers only.

**Line 28.** Net earnings from self-employment for the period.

**Line 29.** Prorated social security tax limit: $44,025 / $73,375 / $117,400 / $176,100.

**Line 30.** Enter actual wages for the period subject to social security tax or the 6.2% portion of the 7.65% railroad retirement (tier 1) tax.

**Line 31.** Subtract line 30 from line 29. If zero or less, enter -0-.

**Line 32.** Annualization amounts: 0.496, 0.2976, 0.186, 0.124.

**Line 33.** Multiply line 32 by the **smaller** of line 28 or line 31.

**Line 34.** Annualization amounts: 0.116, 0.0696, 0.0435, 0.029.

**Line 35.** Multiply line 28 by line 34.

**Line 36.** Add lines 33 and 35. Enter here and on line 15 above.
