### Specific Instructions

#### Rounding Off to Whole Dollars

You may round amounts to whole dollars. Drop amounts under 50 cents; round amounts from 50 to 99 cents up to the next dollar ($1.39 becomes $1; $2.50 becomes $3). If adding several amounts, you may include cents in the calculation and round only the final total.

#### Capital Loss Carryover Worksheet -- Lines 6 and 14

Use this worksheet if your 2024 Schedule D, line 21, shows a loss AND either:

(a) That loss is smaller (less negative) than your 2024 Schedule D, line 16, loss; or
(b) Your 2024 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 15, would be negative if negative amounts were allowed.

If neither (a) nor (b) applies, you have no carryover.

Spouses who file separately after filing jointly can only deduct carryovers from the spouse who actually had the loss.

If you excluded cancelled debt from income in 2024, modifications may apply; see Publication 4681.

#### Disposal of QOF Investment

If you disposed of a QOF investment during the tax year, check the box on Schedule D, page 1, and review Form 8949 reporting requirements. Complete Form 8997, Part III, per Form 8997 instructions.

#### Lines 1a and 8a -- Transactions Not Reported on Form 8949

You may report aggregate totals directly on Schedule D, lines 1a and 8a (excluding collectibles transactions), when ALL of the following apply:

* You received Form 1099-B or Form 1099-DA (or a substitute statement) showing basis was reported to the IRS
* No adjustments appear in boxes 1f or 1g (Form 1099-B) or boxes 1h or 1i (Form 1099-DA)
* Form 1099-B "Ordinary" box 2 is not checked
* Form 1099-DA "Ordinary" box 6 is not checked
* Form 1099-B "QOF" box 3 is not checked
* Form 1099-DA "QOF" box 3b is not checked
* No QOF deferral/termination elections apply
* No basis, gain/loss type, or other adjustments are needed

Form 8949 is not required for such transactions. Statements are not required for e-filed returns, and Form 8453 is not required.

Calculate gain or loss by subtracting column (e) basis from column (d) proceeds. Enter the result in column (h). Negative amounts go in parentheses.

**Example (basis reported to IRS).** Stock held 3 years shows $6,000 proceeds and $2,000 basis on Form 1099-B (or 1099-DA). Box 12 (Form 1099-B) or box 2 (Form 1099-DA) indicates basis was reported to IRS. Enter $6,000 in column (d) on Schedule D, line 8a, $2,000 in column (e), and $4,000 ($6,000 - $2,000) in column (h).

**Combining transactions.** Two similar transactions with $6,000/$2,000 and $5,000/$3,000: combine to $11,000 column (d), $5,000 column (e), $6,000 column (h).

**Example (basis NOT reported to IRS).** Form 1099-B or 1099-DA shows $6,000 proceeds and $2,000 basis but box 12 (Form 1099-B) or box 2 (Form 1099-DA) is unchecked. Do NOT report on lines 1a or 8a. Instead, report on Form 8949 with the appropriate code selection.

**Example (adjustment needed).** Form 1099-B or 1099-DA shows $6,000 proceeds and $2,000 basis, with basis reported to IRS, but the basis is incorrect. Do NOT report on lines 1a or 8a. Report on Form 8949 with adjustments in columns (f), (g), (h).

#### Lines 1b, 2, 3, 8b, 9, and 10, Column (h) -- Transactions Reported on Form 8949

Complete Form 8949 first, then enter totals on the applicable Schedule D lines.

Calculate gain or loss: subtract column (e) basis from column (d) proceeds, then combine with any column (g) adjustments. Enter the result in column (h). Negative amounts go in parentheses.

**Gain example.** Column (d) = $6,000; column (e) = $2,000. Enter $4,000 in column (h).

**Loss example.** Column (d) = $6,000; column (e) = $8,000. Enter ($2,000) in column (h).

**Adjustment example.** Column (d) = $6,000; column (e) = $2,000; column (g) = ($1,000). Enter $3,000 ($6,000 - $2,000 - $1,000) in column (h).

#### Line 13

Enter your total capital gain distributions. See the Capital Gain Distributions section earlier for guidance.

#### Line 18 -- 28% Rate Gain

Check "Yes" on line 17 and complete the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet if:

* Form 8949, Part II, reports collectibles gain or deductible long-term collectibles loss, OR
* Form 8949, Part II, reports a section 1202 exclusion on QSB stock

**Collectibles include:** works of art, rugs, antiques, metals (gold, silver, platinum bullion), gems, stamps, coins, alcoholic beverages, and certain other tangible personal property.

Also include gains from partnership, S corporation, or trust interests (held over one year) attributable to unrealized appreciation of collectibles. Attach statements as required under Regulations section 1.1(h)-1(e).

#### Line 19 -- Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain

Check "Yes" on line 17 and complete the Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain Worksheet if any of the following apply:

* You sold or otherwise disposed of section 1250 property (depreciated real property) held more than 1 year.
* You received installment payments in 2025 for section 1250 property held more than 1 year.
* You received a Schedule K-1 from an estate, trust, partnership, or S corporation showing "unrecaptured section 1250 gain."
* You received Form 1099-DIV or Form 2439 from a REIT or regulated investment company reporting "unrecaptured section 1250 gain."
* You reported a long-term gain from a partnership interest and the partnership owned section 1250 property.

#### Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain Worksheet -- Line 19

**Lines 1 through 3.** If you have multiple properties, complete a separate calculation for each property and enter the combined total on line 3 before proceeding to line 4.

For each property:

* Line 1: Enter the smaller of line 22 or line 24 of Form 4797 (the lesser of depreciation allowed/allowable or total gain).
* Line 2: Subtract any section 1250 ordinary income recapture (line 26g of Form 4797).
* Line 3: The result is the unrecaptured section 1250 gain for that property.

**Line 4 -- Installment sale gain allocation.** For each section 1250 property held more than 1 year and sold on the installment method:

* **Step 1.** Figure the smaller of: (a) depreciation allowed or allowable, or (b) total sale gain. This equals the smaller of line 22 or line 24 on 2025 Form 4797 (or comparable lines for the year of sale).
* **Step 2.** Reduce Step 1 by any section 1250 ordinary income recapture (line 26g of 2025 Form 4797 or comparable line for the year of sale). The result is the total unrecaptured section 1250 gain to be allocated to installment payments.
* **Step 3.** Generally, the entire gain in each installment payment is treated as unrecaptured section 1250 gain until the Step 2 total is fully accounted for. Figure 2025 unrecaptured section 1250 gain as the smaller of: (a) line 26 or line 37 on 2025 Form 6252, or (b) remaining unrecaptured section 1250 gain (total from Step 2 less all prior-year reported amounts, excluding section 1250 ordinary income recapture). If you elected not to treat all gain from payments received after May 6, 1997, and before August 24, 1999, as unrecaptured section 1250 gain, use only the elected amount to reduce remaining gain. Enter the result on line 4.

#### Lines 20 and 21

Use the instructions on Schedule D to complete lines 20 and 21 based on the amounts from the preceding lines.

If Schedule D, line 16, is a gain:

* Complete line 21 to determine whether you need to use the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet or the Schedule D Tax Worksheet.

If Schedule D, line 16, is a loss:

* Line 21: Enter the smaller of the loss on line 16 or ($3,000) (or ($1,500) if married filing separately). This is your allowable capital loss deduction.
* Enter that amount as a loss on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 7.

#### Line 22

Complete line 22 only if you have qualifying amounts on lines 15 and 16 (both are gains) and either:

* Line 15 or line 16 is more than zero, AND
* Lines 18 and 19 are both -0- or blank

If these conditions are met, check "Yes" to use the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet in the Instructions for Form 1040 to figure your tax. Do not complete the rest of Schedule D.

If lines 18 or 19 are more than zero, check "No" and complete the Schedule D Tax Worksheet in the Instructions for Schedule D to figure your tax.
