## Instructions for Schedule D (Form 1040) - Capital Gains and Losses

### Future Developments

For the latest information about developments related to Schedule D and its instructions, such as legislation enacted after they were published, go to IRS.gov/ScheduleD.

### What's New

**Digital asset reporting codes.** The IRS added codes G through L to lines 1b, 2, 3, 8b, 9, and 10 for reporting digital asset transactions. Taxpayers should reference Form 1099-DA and Form 8949 instructions for additional details.

**Qualified Opportunity Fund basis adjustment.** Taxpayers who made QOF deferral elections meeting the 7-year holding threshold may qualify for a basis adjustment. Form 8997 contains comprehensive guidance on this provision.
