#### Small Business (Section 1244) Stock

Ordinary losses on section 1244 stock are reported on Form 4797. The maximum ordinary loss is $50,000 ($100,000 for married filing jointly). Any loss exceeding the maximum is reported on Form 8949.

**Form 8949 reporting:**

1. Column (a): "Capital portion of section 1244 stock loss"
2. Columns (b) and (c): Complete normally
3. Column (d): Full sale price
4. Column (e): Full basis
5. Column (f): Enter "S"
6. Column (g): Enter the Form 4797 loss claimed as a positive number
7. Column (h): Complete per instructions

**Example.** Stock sold for $1,000 with $60,000 basis, held 3 years. Claim $50,000 ordinary loss on Form 4797. Form 8949, Part II: $1,000 column (d), $60,000 column (e), "S" column (f), $50,000 column (g), ($9,000) column (h).

#### Exclusion of Gain on Qualified Small Business (QSB) Stock

Section 1202 allows exclusion of a portion of gain from QSB stock held more than 5 years. The exclusion percentage depends on acquisition date:

* Acquired on or before February 17, 2009: 50% exclusion
* Acquired February 18 through September 27, 2009: 75% exclusion (some periods 60% for empowerment zone stock)
* Acquired after September 27, 2010: 100% exclusion
* Certain empowerment zone business stock acquired between December 22, 2000, and February 17, 2009: 60% exclusion for gain attributable to periods on or before December 31, 2018

**QSB stock requirements:**

1. Stock of a C corporation (not an S corporation)
2. Originally issued after August 10, 1993
3. Issuing corporation was a qualified small business -- a domestic C corporation with $75 million ($50 million if issued on or before July 4, 2025) or less in total gross assets at all times after August 9, 1993, until issuance and immediately after issuance
4. Stock acquired at original issuance (directly, through an underwriter, or in certain exchanges) for money, property (excluding stock), or services
5. Corporation was a C corporation throughout the holding period; at least 80% of assets used in qualified business; not a foreign corporation, DISC, regulated investment company, REIT, REMIC, FASIT, or cooperative

**Qualified business definition.** Excluded businesses: health, law, engineering, accounting, actuarial science, performing arts, consulting, athletics, financial services, brokerage services; any business where the principal asset is employee reputation or skill; banking, insurance, financing, leasing, or investing businesses; farming (including tree operations); businesses using percentage depletion; hotel, motel, or restaurant operations.

**Empowerment zone business stock.** The 60% exclusion applies when:

1. The stock was of a corporation that was an empowerment zone business during substantially all of the holding period
2. Stock was acquired between December 21, 2000, and February 17, 2009
3. Gain is attributable to periods before December 31, 2019

Stock acquired after February 17, 2009, through September 27, 2010: 75% exclusion. After September 27, 2010: 100% exclusion. See section 1397C for empowerment zone business details.

**Pass-through entities.** Partnership, S corporation, common trust fund, or regulated investment company members must have held their interests from the entity's stock acquisition through the sale to qualify for the exclusion.

**Reporting QSB stock sales.** Report on Form 8949, Part II. Enter "Q" in column (f) and the excluded gain as a negative number in column (g) (in parentheses).

For the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, line 2, enter as a positive number:

* For 50% exclusion: the full exclusion amount
* For 60% exclusion: 2/3 of the exclusion
* For 75% exclusion: 1/3 of the exclusion
* For 100% exclusion: enter -0-

**Form 1099-DIV gains.** If Form 1099-DIV box 2c shows gain that may qualify for section 1202 exclusion: report the total gain (box 2a) on Schedule D, line 13. On Form 8949, Part II, enter the corporation name in column (a), "Q" in column (f), and the excluded gain as a negative number in column (g). For the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, line 2, enter the positive allowable exclusion (50%: full; 60%: 2/3; 75%: 1/3; 100%: -0-).

**Form 2439 gains.** If Form 2439 box 1c shows gain that may qualify for section 1202 exclusion: report the total gain (box 1a) on Schedule D, line 11. On Form 8949, Part II, enter the corporation name in column (a), "Q" in column (f), and the excluded gain as a negative number in column (g). For the 28% Rate Gain Worksheet, line 2, enter the positive allowable exclusion.

**Installment sale QSB stock.** Report on Form 6252. Enter long-term gain on Schedule D, line 11. Calculate the allowable section 1202 exclusion as: total exclusion x (eligible gain recognized / total eligible gain). Report on Form 8949, Part II with "Q" in column (f) and the yearly allowable exclusion as a negative number in column (g).

**Alternative Minimum Tax.** On Form 6251, line 13: enter 7% of the allowable exclusion for 50%, 60%, or 75% exclusions. Leave blank for 100% exclusion.

#### Rollover of Gain From QSB Stock

If you held QSB stock for more than 6 months, you can elect to defer gain by purchasing replacement QSB stock within 60 days of the sale. Pass-through entities may make this election; members of pass-through entities may also make elections if the entities held stock for the entire period.

Partners in partnerships that sold QSB stock should see Schedule K-1, box 11, and Regulations section 1.1045-1.

Gain is recognized to the extent the sale proceeds exceed the cost of the replacement stock. The replacement stock's basis is reduced by the amount of postponed gain.

The election must be made by the return filing due date (including extensions). Elections on amended returns must be filed within 6 months of the return due date (excluding extensions) with the notation "Filed pursuant to section 301.9100-2."

Report on Form 8949, Part I or II (depending on holding period), with "R" in column (f) and the postponed gain as a negative number in column (g) (in parentheses).

#### Exclusion of Gain From DC Zone Assets

Sellers of District of Columbia Enterprise Zone assets acquired after 1997 and before 2012, held more than 5 years, may exclude qualified capital gain.

**DC Zone assets include:** DC Zone business stock, DC Zone partnership interests, and DC Zone business property.

**Qualified capital gain excludes:**

* Gain attributable to periods after December 31, 2016
* Section 1245 ordinary income recapture
* Section 1250 gain (depreciation-based)
* Real property or intangible asset gain not integral to DC Zone business
* Related-party transaction gain

**Reporting.** For DC Zone business stock or partnership interests: report on Form 8949, Part II. Enter "X" in column (f) and the exclusion as a negative number in column (g). For DC Zone business property: report on Form 4797.

#### Exclusion of Gain From Qualified Community Assets

Sellers of qualified community assets acquired after 2001 and before 2010, held more than 5 years, may exclude qualified capital gain on renewal community business interests or property.

**Qualified community assets include:** qualified community stock, partnership interests, and business property.

**Qualified capital gain excludes:**

* Gain attributable to periods after December 31, 2014
* Section 1245 ordinary income recapture
* Section 1250 gain
* Real property or intangible asset gain not integral to renewal community business
* Related-party transaction gain

**Reporting.** For community stock or partnership interests: report on Form 8949, Part II. Enter "X" in column (f) and the exclusion as a negative number in column (g). For community business property: report on Form 4797.

#### Deferral of Gain Invested in a Qualified Opportunity Fund (QOF)

If you have eligible gain, you can elect to defer part or all of the gain by investing in a QOF within 180 days. Tax is deferred until the earlier of:

* The QOF investment's sale or exchange, or
* December 31, 2026

The deferral applies only to the amount invested in the QOF (up to the full realized gain). Investments held for 10 or more years may qualify for permanent exclusion of post-investment gain.

**QOF definition.** An investment vehicle (corporation or partnership) organized to invest in qualified opportunity zone property.

Form 8997 must be filed for each year you hold a QOF investment at the beginning or end of the tax year, and for any dispositions during the year. Basis adjustments may apply after the 5-year holding period; see Form 8997 instructions.

Report eligible gain normally, then use Form 8949 to make the deferral election.

#### Rollover of Gain From Stock Sold to ESOPs or Certain Cooperatives

If you sell qualified securities held for more than 3 years to an employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) or an eligible worker-owned cooperative, you can postpone gain by purchasing qualified replacement property. See Publication 550 and Form 8949 instructions (columns (f), (g), (h)) for guidance.
