# Form 8863 Part III — Student and Educational Institution Information

Complete a separate Part III on page 2 for **each student** for whom you are claiming either the American Opportunity Credit or Lifetime Learning Credit. Use additional copies of page 2 as needed.

## Lines 20–21: Student Identification

- **Line 20:** Student name as shown on page 1 of your tax return.
- **Line 21:** Student's social security number as shown on page 1 of your tax return.

## Line 22: Educational Institution Information

Two institutions (a and b) can be listed per student.

For each institution:
- **(1)** Name and address (number, street, city, state, ZIP). For foreign addresses, include the country name, foreign province/state, and foreign postal code.
- **(2)** Did the student receive Form 1098-T from this institution for 2025? Check Yes or No.
- **(3)** Did the student receive Form 1098-T from this institution for 2024 with box 7 checked? Check Yes or No.
- **(4)** Enter the institution's EIN if claiming the American Opportunity Credit or if you checked "Yes" in (2) or (3). The EIN is on Form 1098-T or available from the institution.

## AOC Eligibility Questions (Lines 23–26)

These questions determine whether the student qualifies for the AOC. If any answer directs you to "Stop! Go to line 31," the student can only claim the LLC.

- **Line 23:** Has the American opportunity credit been claimed for this student for any 4 prior tax years?
  - [ ] **Yes** — Stop! Go to line 31 for this student (LLC only).
  - [ ] **No** — Go to line 24.

- **Line 24:** Was the student enrolled at least half-time for at least one academic period that began or is treated as having begun in 2025 at an eligible educational institution in a program leading toward a postsecondary degree, certificate, or other recognized postsecondary educational credential?
  - [ ] **Yes** — Go to line 25.
  - [ ] **No** — Stop! Go to line 31 for this student (LLC only).

- **Line 25:** Did the student complete the first 4 years of postsecondary education before 2025?
  - [ ] **Yes** — Stop! Go to line 31 for this student (LLC only).
  - [ ] **No** — Go to line 26.

- **Line 26:** Was the student convicted, before the end of 2025, of a felony for possession or distribution of a controlled substance?
  - [ ] **Yes** — Stop! Go to line 31 for this student (LLC only).
  - [ ] **No** — Complete lines 27 through 30 for this student.

## American Opportunity Credit Computation (Lines 27–30)

**You cannot take the AOC and the LLC for the same student in the same year.** If you complete lines 27–30, do not complete line 31 for this student.

- **Line 27:** Adjusted qualified education expenses. **Do not enter more than $4,000.** See the Adjusted Qualified Education Expenses Worksheet below.
- **Line 28:** Subtract $2,000 from line 27. If zero or less, enter -0-.
- **Line 29:** Multiply line 28 by 25% (0.25).
- **Line 30:** If line 28 is zero, enter the amount from line 27. Otherwise, add $2,000 to the amount on line 29 and enter the result. Skip line 31. Include the total of all amounts from all Parts III, line 30, on Part I, line 1.

### AOC Credit Computation Summary
- 100% of the first $2,000 of adjusted qualified expenses
- 25% of the next $2,000 of adjusted qualified expenses
- Maximum per student: $2,500

## Lifetime Learning Credit (Line 31)

- **Line 31:** Adjusted qualified education expenses (see Adjusted Qualified Education Expenses Worksheet). Include the total of all amounts from all Parts III, line 31, on Part II, line 10.

## Adjusted Qualified Education Expenses Worksheet

| Line | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Total qualified education expenses paid in 2025 for the student |
| 2a | Tax-free educational assistance received in 2025 for the student |
| 2b | Tax-free educational assistance received in 2026 (before filing) for 2025 expenses |
| 2c | Refunds of qualified expenses received in 2025 or 2026 (before filing) |
| 3 | Add lines 2a, 2b, and 2c |
| 4 | Adjusted qualified education expenses: line 1 minus line 3. If zero or less, enter -0-. |

Tax-free educational assistance includes: tax-free scholarships and fellowships, Pell grants, employer-provided educational assistance, veterans' educational assistance, and any other nontaxable (tax-free) payments (other than gifts or inheritances) received as educational assistance.
