# Form 8863 — Education Credits (American Opportunity and Lifetime Learning Credits)

## Purpose

Use Form 8863 to figure and claim your education credits — the American Opportunity Credit (AOC) and the Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC). Attach Form 8863 to Form 1040 or 1040-SR.

## Who Can Claim an Education Credit

You may be able to claim an education credit if you, your spouse, or a dependent you claim on your tax return was a student enrolled at or attending an eligible educational institution. The credits are based on adjusted qualified education expenses you paid for the student.

## Who Cannot Claim an Education Credit

You **cannot** claim an education credit if any of the following apply:

- Your filing status is **married filing separately (MFS)**.
- You are listed as a dependent on another person's tax return.
- You (or your spouse, if MFJ) were a nonresident alien for any part of the tax year and did not elect to be treated as a resident alien for tax purposes.
- Your modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) is $90,000 or more ($180,000 or more if married filing jointly).

## Credit Comparison

| Feature | American Opportunity Credit (AOC) | Lifetime Learning Credit (LLC) |
|---------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Maximum credit | $2,500 per eligible student | $2,000 per return |
| Refundable? | Yes — 40% (up to $1,000) | No |
| Number of years available | 4 tax years per student | Unlimited |
| Enrollment requirement | At least half-time, one academic period | One or more courses |
| Degree required? | Yes — must be in degree/credential program | No — includes job-skills courses |
| Felony drug conviction | Disqualifies student | Does not disqualify |
| Qualified expenses | Tuition, required fees, course materials | Tuition and required fees only (books only if required to be paid to institution) |

## MAGI Phase-Out (Both Credits)

Both credits use the same MAGI phase-out ranges:

| Filing Status | Phase-out begins | Phase-out complete (credit = $0) |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| Single, HOH, QSS | $80,000 | $90,000 |
| Married filing jointly | $160,000 | $180,000 |
| Married filing separately | Not eligible — cannot claim either credit |

**MAGI** for this purpose is Form 1040 line 11b (adjusted gross income), modified by adding back any amounts excluded under Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income), Form 4563, or income from Puerto Rico sources.

## Key Cross-References

- Part I, line 8 (refundable AOC) → **Form 1040, line 29**
- Part II, line 19 (nonrefundable education credits) → **Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 3**
- Credit Limit Worksheet uses **Form 1040, line 18** (tax) and **Schedule 3, lines 1, 2, 6d, 6l**

## Form Structure

- **Part III** (page 2, per student): Student info, institution, eligibility questions, AOC or LLC computation per student — **complete first**
- **Part I** (page 1, lines 1–8): Refundable American Opportunity Credit — aggregates AOC across students, applies MAGI phase-out
- **Part II** (page 1, lines 9–19): Nonrefundable Education Credits — combines AOC + LLC after phase-out, applies credit limit
