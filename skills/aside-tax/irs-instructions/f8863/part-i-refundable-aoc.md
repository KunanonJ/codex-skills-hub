# Form 8863 Part I — Refundable American Opportunity Credit (Lines 1–8)

Complete Part III for each student **before** completing Part I.

## Line-by-Line Instructions

- **Line 1:** After completing Part III for each student, enter the total of all amounts from all Parts III, line 30. This is the sum of tentative AOC amounts across all eligible students.

- **Line 2:** Enter the MAGI threshold:
  - $180,000 if married filing jointly
  - $90,000 if single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse

- **Line 3:** Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11b (adjusted gross income). If you are filing Form 2555 or 4563, or excluding income from Puerto Rico, see Pub. 970 for the amount to enter instead.

- **Line 4:** Subtract line 3 from line 2. If zero or less, **stop** — you cannot take any education credit.

- **Line 5:** Enter the phase-out range:
  - $20,000 if married filing jointly
  - $10,000 if single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse

- **Line 6:** Phase-out ratio:
  - If line 4 is **equal to or more than** line 5, enter **1.000** on line 6.
  - If line 4 is **less than** line 5, divide line 4 by line 5. Enter the result as a decimal rounded to at least three places.

- **Line 7:** Multiply line 1 by line 6.
  - **Caution:** If you were under age 24 at the end of the year **and** meet **all** of the following conditions, you cannot take the refundable American opportunity credit: skip line 8, enter the amount from line 7 on line 9, and check the box on line 7.
    1. You were (a) under age 18, or (b) age 18 and your earned income was less than one-half of your support, or (c) age 19–23, a full-time student, and your earned income was less than one-half of your support.
    2. At least one of your parents was alive at the end of the year.
    3. You are not filing a joint return for the year.

- **Line 8:** **Refundable American opportunity credit.** Multiply line 7 by 40% (0.40). Enter the amount here and on **Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 29**. Then go to line 9.

## Phase-Out Computation Summary

The phase-out effectively works as:
- MAGI below $80,000 single ($160,000 MFJ): full credit (ratio = 1.000)
- MAGI between $80,000–$90,000 single ($160,000–$180,000 MFJ): partial credit (ratio = (threshold − MAGI) / range)
- MAGI at or above $90,000 single ($180,000 MFJ): no credit (line 4 ≤ 0)

## Refundable vs. Nonrefundable Split

After applying the phase-out:
- **40%** of the phased-out AOC is refundable (line 8) → Form 1040 line 29
- **60%** of the phased-out AOC is nonrefundable (line 9) → flows to Part II
