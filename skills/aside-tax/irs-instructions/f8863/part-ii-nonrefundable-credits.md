# Form 8863 Part II — Nonrefundable Education Credits (Lines 9–19)

## Line-by-Line Instructions

- **Line 9:** Subtract line 8 from line 7. This is the nonrefundable portion of the AOC. Enter here and on **line 2** of the Credit Limit Worksheet.

- **Line 10:** After completing Part III for each student, enter the total of all amounts from all Parts III, line 31. This is the sum of LLC qualified expenses across all students. If zero, skip lines 11 through 17, enter -0- on line 18, and go to line 19.

- **Line 11:** Enter the smaller of line 10 or $10,000.

- **Line 12:** Multiply line 11 by 20% (0.20). This is the tentative LLC before phase-out.

- **Line 13:** Enter the MAGI threshold:
  - $180,000 if married filing jointly
  - $90,000 if single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse

- **Line 14:** Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11b (adjusted gross income). If filing Form 2555 or 4563, or excluding income from Puerto Rico, see Pub. 970 for the amount to enter.

- **Line 15:** Subtract line 14 from line 13. If zero or less, skip lines 16 and 17, enter -0- on line 18, and go to line 19.

- **Line 16:** Enter the phase-out range:
  - $20,000 if married filing jointly
  - $10,000 if single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse

- **Line 17:** Phase-out ratio:
  - If line 15 is **equal to or more than** line 16, enter **1.000** on line 17 and go to line 18.
  - If line 15 is **less than** line 16, divide line 15 by line 16. Enter the result as a decimal rounded to at least three places.

- **Line 18:** Multiply line 12 by line 17. Enter here and on **line 1** of the Credit Limit Worksheet.

- **Line 19:** **Nonrefundable education credits.** Enter the amount from **line 7** of the Credit Limit Worksheet here and on **Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 3**.

## Credit Limit Worksheet

The nonrefundable education credits (line 19) cannot exceed your tax liability after certain other credits. Use this worksheet to determine the limit.

| Line | Instruction |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | Enter the amount from Form 8863, line 18 (tentative LLC after phase-out) |
| 2 | Enter the amount from Form 8863, line 9 (nonrefundable AOC portion) |
| 3 | Add lines 1 and 2 |
| 4 | Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 18 (tax before credits) |
| 5 | Enter the total of credits from Schedule 3, lines 1, 2, 6d, and 6l |
| 6 | Subtract line 5 from line 4. If zero or less, enter -0-. |
| 7 | Enter the **smaller** of line 3 or line 6. This is your nonrefundable education credit. Enter on Form 8863, line 19, and on Schedule 3, line 3. |

## LLC Computation Summary

- 20% of up to $10,000 adjusted qualified education expenses = $2,000 maximum per return
- Same MAGI phase-out as AOC: $80K–$90K single, $160K–$180K MFJ
- The LLC is entirely nonrefundable (limited by tax liability via the Credit Limit Worksheet)
