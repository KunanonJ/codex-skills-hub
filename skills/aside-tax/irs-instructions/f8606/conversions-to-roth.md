#### Part II -- 2025 Conversions From Traditional, SEP, or SIMPLE IRAs to Roth IRAs

Complete Part II if you converted part or all of your traditional, SEP, or SIMPLE IRAs to a Roth IRA in 2025.

##### Line 16

If you didn't complete line 8, see the instructions for that line. Then, enter on line 16 the amount you would have entered on line 8 had you completed it.

##### Line 17

If you didn't complete line 11, enter on line 17 the amount from line 2 (or the amount you would have entered on line 2 if you had completed that line) plus any contributions included on line 1 that you made before the conversion.

##### Line 18

If your entry on line 18 is zero or less, don't include the result on 2025 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 4b. Include the full amount of the distribution on 2025 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 4a.
