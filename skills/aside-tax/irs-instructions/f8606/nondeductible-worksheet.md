**2025 Line 15c Worksheet**

Complete this worksheet if a qualified birth or adoption distribution, emergency personal expense distribution, domestic abuse distribution, or terminal illness distribution was made to you in 2025 from a traditional IRA and you repaid part or all of the distribution within the 3-year repayment period.

The amount on line 5 of the worksheet is the taxable income you will enter on line 15c of your 2025 Form 8606. The amount, if any, on line 6 of the worksheet is the amount you will include on line 14 of your Form 8606 in the next year after 2025 you report traditional IRA distributions on Form 8606.

**Caution.** If you make the repayment in a year after the distribution, you may need to file an amended return.

You do **not** have to complete the worksheet if:

- You didn't report any qualified birth or adoption distributions, emergency personal expense distributions, domestic abuse distributions, or terminal illness distributions on line 7 of your 2025 Form 8606; or
- You reported such distributions on line 7 but you didn't repay any of the distributions within the 3-year repayment period.

**Tip.** If 2025 Form 8606, line 15a, is zero, enter -0- on 2025 Form 8606, line 15c. You do not need to complete this worksheet.

###### Example 1 -- Line 15c Worksheet

In July 2025, a $7,500 distribution, unrelated to a qualified disaster or special distribution type, was made to you from your traditional IRA. On August 31, 2025, your child was born. In September 2025, a $5,000 qualified birth or adoption distribution was made to you from your traditional IRA. Total distributions reported on Form 8606, line 7: $12,500. Form 8606, line 15a: $10,000. In December 2025, you repaid $3,000 of the qualified birth or adoption distribution.

Worksheet completion:

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| 1 | $10,000 |
| 2 | $12,500 |
| 3a | $5,000 |
| 3b | 0.4 ($5,000 / $12,500) |
| 3c | $4,000 ($10,000 x 0.4) |
| 3d | $3,000 |
| 3e | $3,000 (smaller of $4,000 and $3,000) |
| 4 | $10,000 ($10,000 - $0) |
| 5 | $7,000 ($10,000 - $3,000) |
| 6 | (blank -- line 3d is not more than line 3e) |

Because you have entered $7,000 on line 5 of the worksheet, you will enter $7,000 on line 15c of your 2025 Form 8606 and include $7,000 on line 4b of your 2025 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, as applicable.

###### Example 2 -- Line 15c Worksheet

Same facts as Example 1, except in December 2025, you repaid the entire $5,000 qualified birth or adoption distribution.

Worksheet completion:

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| 1 | $10,000 |
| 2 | $12,500 |
| 3a | $5,000 |
| 3b | 0.4 ($5,000 / $12,500) |
| 3c | $4,000 ($10,000 x 0.4) |
| 3d | $5,000 |
| 3e | $4,000 (smaller of $4,000 and $5,000) |
| 4 | $10,000 ($10,000 - $0) |
| 5 | $6,000 ($10,000 - $4,000) |
| 6 | $1,000 ($5,000 - $4,000) |

Because you have entered $6,000 on line 5 of the worksheet, you will enter $6,000 on line 15c of your 2025 Form 8606 and include $6,000 on line 4b of your 2025 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, as applicable.

Because you have entered $1,000 on line 6 of the worksheet, you will include $1,000 on line 14 of your Form 8606 for the next year after 2025 you report traditional IRA distributions in Part I of Form 8606.

###### Example 3 -- Line 15c Worksheet

Same facts as Example 1, except the repayment of $3,000 was made in December 2026 instead of December 2025. You timely filed your 2025 Form 1040 on April 6, 2026, with your 2025 Form 8606 attached, reporting $10,000 on line 4b and line 15c. On December 16, 2026, you filed an amended 2025 Form 8606.

Because you repaid $3,000 of the 2025 qualified birth or adoption distribution within the 3-year repayment period, you must complete a 2025 Line 15c Worksheet to determine the amount to place on line 15c of your 2025 Form 8606 and to include on line 14 of your Form 8606 for the next year after 2025 you report traditional IRA distributions on Form 8606.

Worksheet completion yields the same results as Example 1: Line 5 = $7,000, Line 6 = blank.

Because you have entered $7,000 on line 5 of the worksheet, you will enter $7,000 on line 15c of your amended 2025 Form 8606 and include $7,000 on line 4b of your amended 2025 Form 1040. You will need to file an amended 2025 Form 1040 to report the amount from your amended 2025 Form 8606.

###### Example 4 -- Line 15c Worksheet

In July 2025, a $7,500 domestic abuse distribution was made to you from your traditional IRA (maximum allowed under the plan is $10,000 or 50% of the nonforfeitable benefit of $30,000, whichever is less). On August 31, 2025, your child was born. In September 2025, a $5,000 qualified birth or adoption distribution was made to you from your traditional IRA. Total distributions reported on Form 8606, line 7: $12,500. Form 8606, line 15a: $8,000. In December 2025, you repaid $3,000 of the qualified birth or adoption distribution.

Worksheet completion:

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| 1 | $8,000 |
| 2 | $12,500 |
| 3a | $12,500 (total of both domestic abuse and birth/adoption distributions) |
| 3b | 1.0 ($12,500 / $12,500) |
| 3c | $8,000 ($8,000 x 1.0) |
| 3d | $3,000 |
| 3e | $3,000 (smaller of $8,000 and $3,000) |
| 4 | $8,000 ($8,000 - $0) |
| 5 | $5,000 ($8,000 - $3,000) |
| 6 | (blank -- line 3d is not more than line 3e) |

Because you have entered $5,000 on line 5 of the worksheet, you will enter $5,000 on line 15c of your 2025 Form 8606 and include $5,000 on line 4b of your 2025 Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, as applicable.
