# Schedule 8812 — Credits for Qualifying Children and Other Dependents

## Purpose

Use Schedule 8812 (Form 1040) to figure the child tax credit (CTC), the credit for other dependents (ODC), and the additional child tax credit (ACTC). Attach Schedule 8812 to Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR.

## Who Must File

File Schedule 8812 if you are claiming the child tax credit, credit for other dependents, or additional child tax credit. You must have at least one qualifying child or dependent listed in the Dependents section of Form 1040.

## Credit Amounts for 2025

| Credit | Amount per qualifying individual |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Child Tax Credit (CTC) | $2,200 per qualifying child under age 17 |
| Credit for Other Dependents (ODC) | $500 per other dependent |
| Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC) | Up to $1,700 per qualifying child (refundable) |

## SSN Requirements (Beginning 2025)

- **To claim CTC or ACTC:** You (or at least one spouse if filing jointly) must have a valid SSN that is valid for employment and issued before the due date of your return (including extensions). The qualifying child must also have a valid SSN issued before the due date.
- **To claim ODC:** You (and your spouse if filing jointly) must have an SSN or ITIN. The dependent must have an SSN, ITIN, or ATIN issued on or before the due date.

## Income Phase-Out

Credits are reduced if modified AGI (line 3) exceeds:

| Filing Status | Threshold |
|---------------|-----------|
| Married filing jointly | $400,000 |
| All other filing statuses | $200,000 |

The reduction is $50 for each $1,000 (or fraction thereof) over the threshold.

## Form Structure

- **Part I** — Child Tax Credit and Credit for Other Dependents (lines 1–14)
- **Part II-A** — Additional Child Tax Credit for All Filers (lines 15–20)
- **Part II-B** — Certain Filers Who Have Three or More Qualifying Children and Bona Fide Residents of Puerto Rico (lines 21–26)
- **Part II-C** — Additional Child Tax Credit result (line 27)

## Key Cross-References

- Line 14 (CTC + ODC) → **Form 1040, line 19**
- Line 27 (ACTC) → **Form 1040, line 28**
- Credit Limit Worksheet A requires **Form 1040, line 18** (tax before credits)
- Part II-B references **Schedule 1, line 15**; **Schedule 2, lines 5, 6, 13**; **Form 1040, line 27a**; **Schedule 3, line 11**

## Caution: Form 2555 Filers

If you file Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income), you **cannot** claim the additional child tax credit (ACTC).
