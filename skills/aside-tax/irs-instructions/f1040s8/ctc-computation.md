# Schedule 8812 — Part I: Child Tax Credit and Credit for Other Dependents

## Line-by-Line Instructions

### Line 1
Enter the amount from line 11a of your Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR. This is your adjusted gross income (AGI).

### Line 2a
Enter income from Puerto Rico that you excluded. This applies only if you are a bona fide resident of Puerto Rico who excluded income under section 933.

### Line 2b
Enter the amounts from lines 45 and 50 of your Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion).

### Line 2c
Enter the amount from line 15 of your Form 4563 (Exclusion of Income for Bona Fide Residents of American Samoa).

### Line 2d
Add lines 2a through 2c.

### Line 3
Add lines 1 and 2d. This is your **modified adjusted gross income** for purposes of the CTC/ODC phase-out.

### Line 4
Enter the number of qualifying children under age 17 with the required social security number. This is the number of dependents for whom you checked the "Child tax credit" box in the Dependents section of Form 1040.

A qualifying child for the CTC must:
1. Be your dependent claimed on the return
2. Be under age 17 at the end of 2025 (born after December 31, 2008)
3. Have a valid SSN (valid for employment, issued before return due date including extensions)
4. Be a U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or U.S. resident alien

### Line 5
Multiply line 4 by **$2,200**. This is the total CTC amount before phase-out.

### Line 6
Enter the number of other dependents, including any qualifying children who are not under age 17 or who do not have the required social security number.

**Caution:** Do not include yourself, your spouse, or anyone who is not a U.S. citizen, U.S. national, or U.S. resident alien. Also, do not include anyone you included on line 4.

### Line 7
Multiply line 6 by **$500**. This is the total credit for other dependents before phase-out.

### Line 8
Add lines 5 and 7. This is the total credit amount before phase-out.

### Line 9
Enter the amount shown below for your filing status:
- **Married filing jointly** — $400,000
- **All other filing statuses** — $200,000

### Line 10
Subtract line 9 from line 3.
- If **zero or less**, enter 0.
- If **more than zero and not a multiple of $1,000**, enter the **next multiple of $1,000**. For example, if the result is $425, enter $1,000; if the result is $1,025, enter $2,000.

### Line 11
Multiply line 10 by **5% (0.05)**. This is the phase-out reduction amount.

### Line 12
Is the amount on line 8 more than the amount on line 11?
- **No.** Stop here. You cannot take the child tax credit, credit for other dependents, or additional child tax credit. Check the "No" box.
- **Yes.** Subtract line 11 from line 8. Enter the result. Check the "Yes" box.

### Line 13
Enter the amount from **Credit Limit Worksheet A** (see [credit-limit-worksheets.md](credit-limit-worksheets.md)).

This worksheet determines the maximum nonrefundable credit allowed based on your tax liability after other credits.

### Line 14
Enter the **smaller** of line 12 or line 13. This is your **child tax credit and credit for other dependents**.

**Enter this amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 19.**

### After Line 14

If the amount on line 12 is more than the amount on line 14, you may be able to take the **additional child tax credit** on Form 1040, line 28. Complete your Form 1040 or Form 1040-SR through line 27a (or Form 1040-NR through line 26), also complete Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 11, before completing Part II-A.
