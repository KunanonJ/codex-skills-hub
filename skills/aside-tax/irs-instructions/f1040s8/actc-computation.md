# Schedule 8812 — Part II: Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC)

## Eligibility

You may claim the ACTC only if:
1. Line 12 (credit after phase-out) exceeds line 14 (credit limited to tax liability)
2. You have qualifying children under 17 with required SSN
3. You do **not** file Form 2555 (Foreign Earned Income)

The ACTC is a **refundable** credit — it can result in a refund even if you owe no tax.

**Note:** The IRS cannot issue refunds before mid-February 2026 for returns that claim the ACTC.

## Part II-A: Additional Child Tax Credit for All Filers

### Line 15
Reserved for future use.

### Line 16a
Subtract line 14 from line 12. This is the excess CTC that could not be used as a nonrefundable credit.

If **zero**, stop here; you cannot take the additional child tax credit.

### Line 16b
Number of qualifying children under age 17 with the required social security number (same count as line 4) multiplied by **$1,700**.

If **zero**, stop here; you cannot claim the additional child tax credit.

**TIP:** The number of children you use for this line is the same as the number of children you used for line 4.

### Line 17
Enter the **smaller** of line 16a or line 16b.

### Line 18a — Earned Income
Enter your earned income. Earned income includes:
- Wages, salaries, and tips (Form 1040 line 1z)
- Net earnings from self-employment (Schedule C, Schedule F, Schedule K-1 Form 1065)
- Statutory employee income (Schedule C from a W-2 with "Statutory employee" checked)
- Nontaxable combat pay (if elected for inclusion)

Earned income does **not** include:
- Income excluded under a tax treaty
- Medicaid waiver payments (unless taxpayer elects inclusion)
- Clergy housing allowance rental value
- Pension or annuity income
- Social Security benefits
- Interest or dividends
- Unemployment compensation

If you had **self-employment income**, use the Earned Income Worksheet:
1. Start with Form 1040/1040-SR/1040-NR, line 1z
2. Add nontaxable combat pay (if electing to include)
3. Add Schedule C statutory employee income
4. Add net profit (or loss) from Schedule C and Schedule K-1 (Form 1065)
5. Add net farm profit (or loss) from Schedule F and K-1 farm income
6. Subtract Medicaid waiver payments excluded from income (unless electing inclusion)
7. Subtract Schedule 1, line 15 (deductible part of SE tax)
8. Result = earned income for line 18a

### Line 18b — Nontaxable Combat Pay
Enter the total nontaxable combat pay received in 2025. This is shown on Form W-2, box 12, code Q, or on Form 1040/1040-SR, line 1i.

### Line 19
Is the amount on line 18a more than $2,500?
- **No.** Leave line 19 blank and enter 0 on line 20. Check the "No" box.
- **Yes.** Subtract $2,500 from the amount on line 18a. Enter the result. Check the "Yes" box.

### Line 20
Multiply the amount on line 19 by **15% (0.15)** and enter the result.

### After Line 20 — Branching Logic

**Next.** On line 16b, is the amount $5,100 or more?

- **No.** If you are a bona fide resident of Puerto Rico, go to line 21. Otherwise, skip Part II-B and enter the **smaller** of line 17 or line 20 on line 27.
- **Yes.** If line 20 is equal to or more than line 17, skip Part II-B and enter the amount from line 17 on line 27. Otherwise, go to line 21.

The $5,100 threshold corresponds to 3 qualifying children ($1,700 x 3 = $5,100). Part II-B provides an alternative computation for filers with 3+ qualifying children or bona fide Puerto Rico residents that may yield a larger ACTC.

## Part II-B: Three or More Qualifying Children and Bona Fide Residents of Puerto Rico

### Line 21
Enter withheld social security, Medicare, and Additional Medicare taxes from Form(s) W-2, boxes 4 and 6. If married filing jointly, include your spouse's amounts with yours.

If your employer withheld or you paid Additional Medicare Tax or tier 1 RRTA taxes, or if you are a bona fide resident of Puerto Rico, use the Additional Medicare Tax and RRTA Tax Worksheet:
1. Social security tax withheld (W-2 box 4; PR Form 499R-2/W-2PR box 21)
2. Medicare tax withheld (W-2 box 6; PR Form 499R-2/W-2PR box 23)
3. Additional Medicare Tax (Form 8959, line 7)
4. One-half Additional Medicare Tax on self-employment (Form 8959, line 13)
5. Any Tier 1 RRTA taxes and corresponding Medicare/Additional Medicare Tax
6. Sum = line 21

### Line 22
Enter the total of the amounts from:
- Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 15 (deductible part of SE tax)
- Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 5 (unreported SS/Medicare tax)
- Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 6 (self-employment tax)
- Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 13 (uncollected SS/Medicare tax on tips or group-term life)

### Line 23
Add lines 21 and 22.

### Line 24
- **Form 1040 and 1040-SR filers:** Enter the total of the amounts from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 27a (EIC), and Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 11 (other payments and refundable credits).
- **Form 1040-NR filers:** Enter the amount from Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 11.

### Line 25
Subtract line 24 from line 23. If zero or less, enter 0.

### Line 26
Enter the **larger** of line 20 or line 25.

**Next**, enter the **smaller** of line 17 or line 26 on line 27.

## Part II-C: Additional Child Tax Credit

### Line 27
This is your **additional child tax credit**.

**Enter this amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 28.**
