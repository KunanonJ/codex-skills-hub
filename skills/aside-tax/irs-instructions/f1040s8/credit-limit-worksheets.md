# Schedule 8812 — Credit Limit Worksheet A

## Purpose

Credit Limit Worksheet A determines the maximum amount of child tax credit and credit for other dependents (nonrefundable portion) that can offset your tax liability. The CTC/ODC is nonrefundable — it can reduce your tax to zero but cannot produce a refund by itself. Any excess may be claimed as the refundable ACTC in Part II.

## Credit Limit Worksheet A

1. Enter the amount from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, **line 18** (tax before credits, including tax from Schedule 2 Part I if applicable).
2. Add the following credits if applicable:
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 1 (Foreign tax credit)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 2 (Child and dependent care credit, Form 2441)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 3 (Education credits, Form 8863)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 4 (Retirement savings credit)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 5a (Residential clean energy credit, Form 5695 Part I)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 5b (Energy efficient home improvement credit, Form 5695 Part II)
   - Schedule 3 (Form 1040), lines 6a through 6m (Other nonrefundable credits)
   Total of all the above credits = Worksheet A, line 2.
3. Subtract Worksheet A line 2 from Worksheet A line 1. If zero or less, enter 0.
4. If you are claiming any of the following credits, **and** you are not filing Form 2555, **and** Schedule 8812 line 4 is greater than zero, enter the amount from **Credit Limit Worksheet B** (see below). Otherwise, enter 0.
   - Mortgage interest credit (Form 8396)
   - Adoption credit (Form 8839)
   - Residential clean energy credit (Form 5695, Part I)
   - DC first-time homebuyer credit (Form 8859)
5. Subtract Worksheet A line 4 from Worksheet A line 3. If zero or less, enter 0.
6. **Enter this amount on Schedule 8812, line 13.**

## Credit Limit Worksheet B

Credit Limit Worksheet B is required **only** when:
- You are claiming the mortgage interest credit, adoption credit, residential clean energy credit (Part I), or DC first-time homebuyer credit; **AND**
- You are **not** filing Form 2555; **AND**
- Schedule 8812, line 4 is greater than zero

### Worksheet B Computation

1. Enter the amount from Schedule 8812, line 12 (credit after phase-out).
2. Enter the amount from Schedule 8812, line 5 (CTC portion only = qualifying children x $2,200). **Note:** Use line 5, not line 8, because this worksheet applies only to the CTC portion.
3. Enter the earned income amount. If using the Earned Income Worksheet from the ACTC instructions (Part II-A), enter that amount. Otherwise, compute earned income per the Earned Income Chart.
4. Is line 3 (earned income) more than $2,500?
   - **No.** Enter 0 on line 5 and go to line 6.
   - **Yes.** Subtract $2,500 from line 3. Enter the result on line 5.
5. Multiply line 4 result by 15% (0.15). Enter the result.
6. Enter the smaller of line 1 or line 2.
7. Enter the larger of line 5 or line 6.
8. Enter the amount from Schedule 8812, line 8.
9. Enter the smaller of line 7 or line 8.
10. Enter the amount from Worksheet A, line 3 (tax minus other credits).
11. Subtract line 9 from line 10. If zero or less, enter 0.
12. **Enter this amount on Credit Limit Worksheet A, line 4.**

## Summary of Flow

```
Form 1040 line 18 (tax)
  minus Schedule 3 nonrefundable credits
  minus Credit Limit Worksheet B (if applicable)
  = Maximum CTC/ODC (Schedule 8812 line 13)

Schedule 8812 line 14 = min(line 12, line 13) → Form 1040 line 19

Excess (line 12 - line 14) → feeds into ACTC computation (Part II)
```
