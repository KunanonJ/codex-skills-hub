## Instructions for Form 8889 - Health Savings Accounts (HSAs)

### Future Developments

For the latest information about developments related to Form 8889 and its instructions, such as legislation enacted after they were published, go to IRS.gov/Form8889.

### What's New

P.L. 119-21 (July 4, 2025) amended Code section 223 to:
1. Allow HSA eligible individuals to have disregarded coverage for telehealth and remote care.
2. Permit HDHPs to exclude deductibles for telehealth services without losing HDHP status.

These provisions apply to plan years beginning after 2024.

### Reminders

**Preventive Care Expansion (Notice 2024-75).**
- Over-the-counter oral contraceptives and male condoms added to preventive care list.
- All breast cancer screening types for undiagnosed individuals treated as preventive care.
- Continuous glucose monitors for diabetics generally treated as preventive care.
- Insulin product safe harbor applies regardless of diabetes diagnosis status.

**Condom Coverage (Notice 2024-71).**
- Amounts paid for condoms qualify as medical expenses under IRC section 213(d).
- Eligible for HSA, health FSA, Archer MSA, and HRA reimbursement.
- Cannot be deducted under section 213 if paid or reimbursed through health plans.

**Additional Resources.** Consult IRS.gov for FAQs on nutrition, wellness, and general health expenses related to HSAs.
