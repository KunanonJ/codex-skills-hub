### Specific Instructions

#### Line 1 -- Trade, Business, or Aggregation Name and Identification

**Line 1(a).** Enter the name of the trade, business, or aggregation.

  * If aggregating multiple businesses, enter "Aggregation 1," "Aggregation 2," etc., instead of individual names, and leave line 1(b) blank.
  * **Note.** Attach Schedule B (Form 8995-A) when aggregating.
  * If using the Rev. Proc. 2019-38 safe harbor for rental real estate, enter "Enterprise 1," "Enterprise 2," etc.

**Line 1(b).** Enter the employer identification number (EIN). If there is no EIN, enter the Social Security number (SSN) or individual taxpayer identification number (ITIN). For a sole-owned LLC that is not treated as a separate entity, enter the LLC's EIN or the owner's identification number.

**Line 1(c).** Enter the net QBI or loss for the trade, business, or aggregation. Exclude suspended losses and losses or deductions suspended by other code provisions that are allowed in the current year.

#### Line 2 -- Additional Trades or Businesses

If you have more than five trades or businesses, attach a statement listing the names, taxpayer identification numbers, and income or loss amounts, and include the totals on line 2.

#### Line 3 -- Qualified Business Net Loss Carryforward

Include any qualified trade or business loss carryforward allowed in the current year taxable income calculation, even if the business no longer exists. See the "Determining Your Qualified Business Income" section and the "Tracking Losses or Deductions Suspended by Other Provisions" section. Separately track losses remaining suspended by other code provisions.

#### Line 4 -- Qualified Business Net Income (Loss)

If you have a qualified business net loss, you do not qualify for the QBI deduction unless you have qualified REIT dividends or qualified PTP income. Losses carry forward to the next year without affecting deductibility under other code provisions.

#### Line 6 -- Qualified REIT Dividends

Enter income as a positive number. Enter losses as a negative number.

#### Line 7 -- Qualified PTP Net Income (Loss) Carryforward

Include the qualified portion of PTP loss carryforward allowed in the current year taxable income calculation, even if you no longer hold the PTP interest or it no longer exists. Separately track losses remaining suspended by other code provisions.

#### Line 8 -- Qualified PTP Net Income (Loss)

Negative amounts carry forward to the next year without affecting deductibility under other code provisions.

#### Line 11 -- Taxable Income Before QBI Deduction

Calculate as follows based on your return type:

**Form 1040 or 1040-SR filers:** Line 11a minus lines 12e and 13b.

**Form 1040-NR filers:** Line 11a minus lines 12, 13b, and 13c.

**Form 1041 filers (Estates/Trusts):** Line 17 minus lines 18, 19, and 21.

**Form 1041-N filers:** Line 5 minus line 12 (except any QBI on line 9).

**Form 990-T filers (Tax-Exempt Organizations):** Part I, line 7, minus line 8.

**ESBT S-Corporation Portion:** ESBT Tax Worksheet, line 5 minus lines 6 through 10.

#### Line 12 -- Net Capital Gain

Report based on your return type:

**Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR.** Line 3a plus net capital gain. If Schedule D is not required, net capital gain is Form 1040/1040-SR/1040-NR, line 7a. If Schedule D is filed, net capital gain is the smaller of Schedule D, line 15 or 16 (treat as zero if either is a loss or zero).

**Form 1041 (Estates/Trusts).** Line 2b(2) plus net capital gain. If Schedule D is required, add qualified dividends to the smaller of Schedule D, line 18a(2) or 19(2) (treat as zero if either is a loss or zero).

**Form 1041-N.** Line 2b plus the smaller of Schedule D, line 10 or 11 (treat as zero if either is a loss or zero).

**Form 990-T (Tax-Exempt Trusts).** Schedule D (Form 1041), the smaller of line 18a(2) or 19(2) (treat as zero if either is a loss or zero).

**ESBT S-Corporation Portion.** ESBT Tax Worksheet, line 2b, plus the smaller of Schedule D (Form 1041), line 18a(2) or 19(2) (treat as zero if either is a loss or zero).

#### Line 15 -- QBI Deduction

Enter this amount on your tax return as follows:

  * Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR: line 13a
  * Form 1041: line 20
  * Form 1041-N: line 9
  * Form 990-T: line 9
  * ESBT S-Corporation portion: line 11

#### Line 16 -- Qualified Business Net (Loss) Carryforward

This amount carries forward to the next year, offsetting QBI in later years regardless of whether the trade(s) or business(es) that generated it still exists. This carryforward does not affect deductibility under other code provisions.

#### Line 17 -- Qualified PTP Net (Loss) Carryforward

This amount carries forward to the next year, offsetting qualified REIT dividends and qualified PTP income in later years regardless of whether the PTP(s) still exists. This carryforward does not affect deductibility under other code provisions.
