#### How To Report an Election To Defer Tax on Eligible Gain Invested in a Qualified Opportunity Fund (QOF)

**Tip:** For more information on QOFs, see Pub. 544.

If you elect to defer tax on an eligible gain by investing in a QOF, report the eligible gain on the form and in the manner otherwise instructed. For example, individual taxpayers would report gain from the sale of stock on Form 8949 and Schedule D (Form 1040). If the gain is reported on Form 8949, do not make any adjustments for the deferral in column (g).

Report the deferral of the eligible gain on its own row of Form 8949 in Part I with box C or box I checked or Part II with box F or box L checked (depending on whether the gain being deferred is short term or long term). If you made multiple investments in different QOFs or in the same QOF on different dates, use a separate row for each investment. If you invested eligible gains of the same character (but from different transactions) on the same date into the same QOF, you can group those investments on the same row. In column (a), enter only the employer identification number (EIN) of the QOF into which you invested. In column (b), enter the date you invested in the QOF. Leave columns (c), (d), and (e) blank. Enter code "Z" in column (f) and the amount of the deferred gain as a negative number (in parentheses) in column (g).

Your investment in a QOF can be used to defer only long-term gain, only short-term gain, or a combination of both short-term and long-term gain. You do not need to trace or allocate the funds invested in a QOF to the specific gain being deferred, but the investment in the QOF must have occurred within the 180-day period beginning on the date the deferred gain was realized. If you realized both short-term and long-term gains during the 180-day period, you can choose how much of each gain to defer by reporting the deferral in Part I or Part II, as applicable. The character of the eligible gain will survive the investment in the QOF. When you recognize the eligible gain (either when you sell or exchange or otherwise dispose of your investment in the QOF or December 31, 2026, whichever is earlier), the gain you recognize will be the same character as the gain you deferred.

**Tip:** If you are attaching multiple Forms 8949 to your return, attach the Form(s) 8949 that lists code "Z" in column (f) first.

**Tip:** If you elect to defer tax on an eligible gain by investing in a QOF, you will need to complete a Form 8997 for each year you hold the investment and for the year you dispose of the investment. If you have held that investment for more than 5 years, see the Instructions for Form 8997 for additional information regarding the basis of that investment.

#### How To Report Eligible Gains From Section 1231 Property

Taxpayers deferring eligible gains from section 1231 property, including gains from installment sales and like-kind exchanges, by investing in a QOF must report the deferral election on Form 8949 in the tax year of the deferral. Likewise, taxpayers selling or exchanging a QOF investment must report the inclusion of the eligible gain on Form 8949.

##### Reporting the Deferral Election

Each QOF investment of section 1231 gains will use two separate rows in Part II (long-term). On the first row, enter the description of the section 1231 property sold or exchanged (with gains being deferred) in column (a). Complete columns (b) and (c) with the dates of acquisition and disposition. Enter the gain amount in both columns (d) and (h). Leave columns (e) and (g) blank. In column (f), do not enter code "Z."

On the second row, enter only the EIN of the QOF in column (a). In column (b), enter the date you invested in the QOF. Leave columns (c), (d), and (e) blank. In column (f), enter "Z." In column (g), enter the deferred gain as a negative number (in parentheses). Leave column (h) blank.

For example, if you sold real property and realized a gain of $50,000 and you want to defer the tax on this gain by investing the entire $50,000 in a QOF, you would report both transactions on Form 8949 as follows:

- First row: Real property description, with date acquired and date sold, $50,000 gain in columns (d) and (h), and nothing in columns (e), (f), and (g).
- Second row: QOF EIN only in column (a), investment date in column (b), "Z" in column (f), and ($50,000) in column (g). Leave columns (c), (d), (e), and (h) blank.

##### Reporting the Inclusion

When you sell or exchange your investment in a QOF or December 31, 2026, whichever is earlier, report the deferred gain in two parts on Form 8949. Include all of your transactions in the computation of long-term gain (or loss) and include both the original sale or exchange transactions that produced the deferred gain and the transactions relating to the disposition of the QOF investment.

On the first row, enter a description of the QOF investment sold or exchanged in column (a). Enter the sale date in column (c). Enter the proceeds from the sale in column (d). Enter the basis in column (e). Calculate the gain or loss and enter the result in column (h). Leave column (b) blank and leave columns (f) and (g) blank unless an adjustment is required by code "Y."

On the second row, enter only the description that cross-references the original transaction in column (a) (for example, "Long-term gain from real property sale entered on Form 8949, Part II, row 1, tax year 2025"). In column (f), enter code "Y." In column (g), enter the deferred gain as a positive number. Leave columns (b), (c), (d), (e), and (h) blank.

For the example above, if you sold your QOF investment for $55,000 and your basis in the investment was $50,000, the second year transaction would be reported as follows:

- First row: Description of QOF investment sold, date sold in column (c), $55,000 in column (d), $50,000 in column (e), and $5,000 gain in column (h). Leave columns (b), (f), and (g) blank.
- Second row: A description cross-referencing the original transaction in column (a), code "Y" in column (f), and $50,000 (the deferred gain amount) as a positive number in column (g). Leave columns (b), (c), (d), (e), and (h) blank.

#### How To Report Gain Previously Deferred in a QOF Investment

If you disposed of a QOF investment during the current tax year, report the disposition and the deferred gain. You will use code "Y" in column (f) when reporting the gain previously deferred in a QOF investment. Form 8997, line 3 will provide the amount of the deferred gain that must be included.

On the first row of Form 8949, Part II, enter the name of the QOF in column (a), the date you invested in the QOF in column (b), and the date of disposition of the QOF investment in column (c). In column (d), enter the proceeds from the disposition. In column (e), enter the basis in the QOF investment (the amount you invested). Calculate the gain or loss from the disposition and enter it in column (h). Leave columns (f) and (g) blank.

On the second row, enter the description that corresponds to the original sale or exchange transaction in column (a) (for example, "Gain from sale of real property on 1/1/2025 to be included in income from Form 8997, line 3"). Leave columns (b) and (c) blank. In column (f), enter code "Y." In column (g), enter the amount shown on Form 8997, line 3, as a positive number. Leave columns (d), (e), and (h) blank.

If you need additional information for reporting the disposition of a QOF investment, see the instructions for Form 8997.

#### Line 2

Include all capital losses on your Form 8949. If you have a net capital loss, you can deduct the lesser of the loss or $3,000 (or if married filing separately, $1,500) against other income. Carry forward any excess capital loss.
