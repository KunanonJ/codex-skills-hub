### 2025 Optional State and Local Sales Tax Tables

The full state and local sales tax tables are not reproduced in this local markdown set. Use the official Schedule A instructions when a direct table lookup is required.

---

### Worksheet Summary (State and Local General Sales Tax Deduction Worksheet - Line 5a)

1. **Line 1:** State general sales taxes from the state table (by income bracket and family size)
2. **Line 2:** Base local general sales taxes from local table (for states with local tables: AL, AK, AZ, AR, CO, GA, IL, KS, LA, MS, MO, NY, NC, SC, TN, UT, VA). Enter 0 for other states.
3. **Line 3:** Local general sales tax rate (as percentage, e.g., 2.5 for 2.5%)
4. **Line 4:** State general sales tax rate (only used if line 2 = 0, for ratio method)
5. **Line 5:** Line 3 / Line 4 (ratio, only if line 2 = 0)
6. **Line 6:** If line 2 ≠ 0: line 2 × line 3. If line 2 = 0: line 1 × line 5.
7. **Line 7:** State and local general sales taxes paid on specified items (motor vehicles, boats, etc.)
8. **Line 8:** Line 1 + Line 6 + Line 7 = **total deduction for Schedule A line 5a**

**States without local sales tax** (skip lines 2-5, line 6 = 0): CT, DC, IN, KY, ME, MD, MA, MI, NJ, RI

**States with local tables:** Use the "Which Optional Local Sales Tax Table Should I Use?" chart in the Schedule A instructions to determine which local table applies.

### Income Brackets (shared by all tables)

19 brackets: $0-$20K, $20K-$30K, $30K-$40K, $40K-$50K, $50K-$60K, $60K-$70K, $70K-$80K, $80K-$90K, $90K-$100K, $100K-$120K, $120K-$140K, $140K-$160K, $160K-$180K, $180K-$200K, $200K-$225K, $225K-$250K, $250K-$275K, $275K-$300K, $300K+

### Family Size Columns

6 columns: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, Over 5

---

### Notable State Footnotes

1. States marked with footnote 1 use the Ratio Method (line 5 = local rate / state rate) for local sales tax.
2. Follow the next-page instructions for local sales tax computation.
3. California's 7.25% rate includes a 1.25% uniform local sales tax. Localities with higher rates should use the Ratio Method with denominator 7.25%.
4. States without local general sales tax: CT, DC, IN, KY, ME, MD, MA, MI, NJ, RI.
5. Nevada's 6.85% rate includes a 2.25% uniform local sales tax. Localities with higher rates should use the Ratio Method with denominator 6.85%.
6. Hawaii's 4.0% rate is actually an excise tax, treated as sales tax for this deduction.
