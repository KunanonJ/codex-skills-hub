### Medical and Dental Expenses

Deductible medical and dental expenses are only those exceeding 7.5% of adjusted gross income from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11b.

If you received health savings account or medical savings account distributions in 2025, see Publication 969.

#### Deceased Taxpayer

Certain medical expenses from a deceased taxpayer's estate may be claimed on the final return. See Publication 502 for details.

#### More Information

Publication 502 explains deductible and non-deductible expenses and capital/special care expenses for disabled individuals.

#### Examples of Medical and Dental Payments You Can Include

To the extent unreimbursed, these are deductible:

- Insurance premiums for medical and dental care, including qualified long-term care insurance (reduce by any self-employed health insurance deduction claimed on Schedule 1, line 17).
- Prescription medicines or insulin.
- Services from acupuncturists, chiropractors, dentists, eye doctors, medical doctors, occupational therapists, osteopathic doctors, physical therapists, podiatrists, psychiatrists, psychoanalysts (medical care only), and psychologists.
- Medical examinations, X-ray and laboratory services, insulin treatments.
- Diagnostic tests (full-body scans, pregnancy tests, blood sugar test kits).
- Nursing help, including employment taxes paid.
- Hospital care (including meals and lodging), clinic costs, lab fees.
- Qualified long-term care services.
- Supplemental Medicare insurance (Medicare Part B).
- Medicare Part D insurance premiums.
- Stop-smoking programs and nicotine withdrawal prescription medicines.
- Weight-loss programs treating specific diseases (including obesity) diagnosed by a doctor.
- Medical treatment at addiction centers.
- Medical aids (eyeglasses, contact lenses, hearing aids, braces, crutches, wheelchairs, guide dogs), including maintenance costs.
- Vision correction surgery (laser eye surgery, radial keratotomy).
- Lodging expenses (but not meals) while receiving medical care from a physician in a hospital or related facility, maximum $50 per night per person.
- Ambulance service and medical care travel costs (own car: gas/oil costs or 21 cents per mile; add parking and tolls).
- Breast pump and lactation assistance supplies.
- Personal protective equipment (masks, hand sanitizer, sanitizing wipes) for Coronavirus prevention.

##### Limit on Long-Term Care Premiums

Maximum deductible amounts by age at end of 2025:

| Age at End of 2025 | Maximum Deduction |
|-----|---------|
| 40 or under | $480 |
| 41-50 | $900 |
| 51-60 | $1,800 |
| 61-70 | $4,810 |
| 71 or older | $6,020 |

#### Examples of Medical and Dental Payments You Can't Include

- Diet food costs.
- Cosmetic surgery (unless correcting congenital abnormality, accident/trauma injury, or disfiguring disease).
- Life insurance or income protection policies.
- Medicare tax on wages/tips or self-employment tax.
- Nursing care for healthy babies.
- Illegal operations or drugs.
- Imported drugs unapproved by U.S. FDA.
- Nonprescription medicines (except insulin) including nicotine gum and certain nicotine patches.
- Travel for rest or change recommended by doctor.
- Funeral, burial, or cremation costs.

**Note.** Individuals age 65 or older who voluntarily paid Medicare Part A premiums without Social Security entitlement may deduct them.

#### Line 1 - Medical and Dental Expenses

Enter total medical and dental expenses minus any insurance or other reimbursements.

If advance premium tax credit payments were received or you may qualify for the credit, complete Form 8962 before filling Schedule A, line 1. See Publication 502 for deduction calculation.

**Tip.** Include medical and dental insurance premiums. If claiming self-employed health insurance deduction on Schedule 1, line 17, reduce premiums by that amount.

##### Whose Medical and Dental Expenses Can You Include?

Include medical bills paid in 2025 for:

- Yourself and spouse.
- All claimed dependents.
- Children not claimed as dependents due to divorce/separation rules.
- Individuals exceeding the $5,200 gross income limit or filing joint returns who could otherwise be dependents.
- Individuals not claimable due to being dependent claimants on someone else's 2025 return.

##### Insurance Premiums for Certain Nondependents

Nondependent individuals (such as children under age 27) covered under your family policy may be deductible if they're described above. If family coverage didn't increase premiums when adding this person, full premiums are deductible.

##### Reimbursements

If insurance paid providers directly, deduct only your portion. If reimbursed in 2025 for 2025 expenses, reduce by reimbursement amount. If reimbursed in 2025 for prior-year expenses previously deducted, include reimbursement on Schedule 1, line 8z if the deduction reduced prior-year tax.

##### Cafeteria Plans

Don't deduct amounts already excluded from income. Exclude employer-sponsored health insurance plan premiums unless reported in W-2 box 1. Similarly, exclude medical expenses paid by the plan unless included in W-2 box 1.
