### Casualty and Theft Losses

#### Line 15

Complete and attach Form 4684, enter Form 4684, line 18 amount on line 15 only.

**Caution.** Don't enter net qualified disaster loss from Form 4684, line 15 here. Enter on line 16 instead.

Personal casualty/theft losses from federally declared disasters deductible only if:

1. Each separate casualty/theft loss exceeds $100, and
2. Total yearly losses (reduced by per-casualty $100 limit) exceed 10% of Form 1040/1040-SR line 11b.

See Form 4684 instructions and Publication 547 for details.

### Other Itemized Deductions

#### Line 16 - Increased Standard Deduction Reporting

If having net qualified disaster loss (Form 4684, line 15) and not itemizing:

1. List Form 4684, line 15 amount on dotted line next to line 16 as "Net Qualified Disaster Loss," attach Form 4684.
2. List standard deduction amount on dotted line next to line 16 as "Standard Deduction Claimed With Qualified Disaster Loss."
3. Combine amounts on line 16, enter on Form 1040/1040-SR line 12e.

**Don't enter amounts on other Schedule A lines.** See Publication 976 for increased standard deduction determination.

#### Net Qualified Disaster Loss Reporting

If having net qualified disaster loss (Form 4684, line 15) and itemizing:

List Form 4684, line 15 amount on dotted line next to line 16 as "Net Qualified Disaster Loss," include with miscellaneous deductions on line 16. Attach Form 4684.

**Caution.** Don't include on line 15.

#### Other Itemized Deductions

List type/amount of each expense from following list next to line 16, enter total on line 16. If filing paper return and can't fit expenses on dotted lines, attach statement showing type/amount.

**Caution.** Only listed expenses deductible on line 16 (see Publication 529 for details):

- Gambling losses (includes nonwinning bingo, lottery, raffle tickets), only to extent of Schedule 1 gambling winnings (line 8b).
- Income-producing property casualty/theft losses (including financial scams) from Form 4684, lines 32, 38b, or Form 4797, line 18a.
- Federal estate tax on decedent income.
- Amortizable bond premium deduction (carryforward or pre-October 23, 1986 bonds).
- Contingent/inflation-indexed payment debt instrument ordinary loss (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities).
- Repayment deduction over $3,000 under claim of right (see Publication 525).
- Certain unrecovered pension investment.
- Disabled person impairment-related work expenses.

### Total Itemized Deductions

#### Line 18

If itemizing for state tax/other purposes despite itemized deductions being less than standard deduction, check line 18 box.
