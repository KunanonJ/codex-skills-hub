### Taxes You Paid

#### Taxes You Can't Deduct

- Federal income and most excise taxes.
- Social security, Medicare, FUTA, and RRTA taxes.
- Customs duties.
- Federal estate and gift taxes.
- Certain state and local taxes including gasoline tax, car inspection fees, sidewalk improvement assessments, taxes paid for others, license fees (marriage, driver's, pet).
- Foreign personal or real property taxes.

#### Line 5 - State and Local Taxes

The deduction for state and local taxes generally has a $40,000 ($20,000 if married filing separately) limit. This includes taxes on lines 5a, 5b, and 5c.

##### Safe Harbor for Charitable Contributions Exchanged for State/Local Tax Credits

If making cash contributions to section 170(c) entities receiving state/local tax credits in return, with required charitable deduction reductions, a safe harbor may apply treating some disallowed charitable contributions as state/local tax payments.

Conditions:

1. Make cash contribution to eligible entity.
2. Receive state/local tax credit.
3. Reduce charitable contribution by credit amount.

Qualifying amounts may be included on line 5a, 5b, or 5c. Credits applied to prior/current state/local tax liability or future years' liability qualify. See Treasury Regulation 1.164-3(j) for details.

##### U.S. Territory Taxes

Include U.S. territory-imposed taxes with state/local taxes on lines 5a, 5b, 5c. Exclude U.S. territory taxes allocable to excluded income.

**Tip.** Consider taking a credit for U.S. territory taxes instead of deductions. See Schedule 3 instructions, line 1.

#### Line 5a - State and Local Income Taxes and Sales Taxes

**Caution.** Elect either general sales taxes OR income taxes. Cannot deduct both.

##### State and Local Income Taxes

If not electing general sales tax deduction, include:

- State/local income taxes withheld from 2025 salary (shown on W-2s; may appear on Forms W-2G, 1099-G, 1099-R, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC).
- State/local income taxes paid in 2025 for prior years.
- 2025 state/local estimated tax payments including prior-year refunds credited to 2025 taxes.
- Mandatory contributions to California, New Jersey, New York Nonoccupational Disability Benefit Funds; Rhode Island Temporary Disability Benefit Fund; Washington Supplemental Workmen's Compensation Fund.
- Mandatory contributions to Alaska, California, New Jersey, Pennsylvania state unemployment funds.
- Mandatory contributions to state family leave programs (New Jersey FLI, California Paid Family Leave).

Don't reduce deduction by:

- Expected 2025 state/local income tax refunds or credits.
- Actually received prior-year state/local tax refunds/credits in 2025 (instead report on Schedule 1, line 1).

##### State and Local General Sales Taxes

Elect to deduct state/local general sales taxes instead of income taxes by checking the box on line 5a. Calculate using actual expenses or optional sales tax tables.

##### Actual Expenses Method

Generally deduct actual state/local general sales taxes (including compensating use taxes) paid in 2025 at the general rate.

**Food, clothing, medical supplies.** Deductible even at rates different from general sales tax.

**Motor vehicles.** Deductible at general or different rates (car, motorcycle, motor home, RV, SUV, truck, van, off-road vehicle, leased vehicles). If rates exceed general rate, deduct only general rate equivalent.

**Trade/business items.** Exclude from sales tax deduction; consult relevant business forms for deductibility.

**Refunds.** If receiving 2025 sales tax refunds for 2025 purchases, reduce deduction. If receiving 2025 refunds for prior purchases, don't reduce 2025 deduction; include refund on Schedule 1, line 8z if prior-year deduction reduced tax.

**Caution.** Keep receipts showing general sales taxes paid.

##### Optional Sales Tax Tables

Use 2025 Optional State Sales Tax Tables and Local Sales Tax Tables to calculate deduction. May add state/local general sales taxes on specified items.

Complete the State and Local General Sales Tax Deduction Worksheet or use the Sales Tax Deduction Calculator at IRS.gov/SalesTax.

**Caution.** If married filing separately, both electing sales tax deduction, with spouse using optional tables, both must use tables.

##### Instructions for State and Local General Sales Tax Deduction Worksheet

###### Line 1

If residing in same state for all 2025, enter applicable amount based on 2025 income and family size from 2025 Optional State Sales Tax Table for your state. Read down "At least-But less than" columns for your state, finding line including your 2025 income. If married filing separately, exclude spouse's income.

**Note.** Family size = dependents listed on Form 1040/1040-SR page 1 (plus continuation sheets) plus you and spouse (if joint return). If married not filing jointly, include spouse only under certain circumstances (see Publication 501).

###### Income

2025 income = Form 1040/1040-SR line 11b plus nontaxable items:

- Tax-exempt interest.
- Veterans' benefits.
- Nontaxable combat pay.
- Workers' compensation.
- Nontaxable social security and railroad retirement portions.
- Nontaxable IRA, pension, or annuity distribution portions (exclude rollovers).
- Public assistance payments.

###### What if you lived in more than one state?

1. Look up table amount for each state using stated rules. (No table = zero amount.)
2. Multiply each state's table amount by fraction: numerator = days lived in state during 2025, denominator = 365.
3. If also in locality with local general sales tax, complete separate worksheet for each state using prorated line 1 amount. Otherwise, combine prorated amounts, enter total on single worksheet line 1.

**Example.** Lived in State A January 1-August 31, 2025 (243 days), State B September 1-December 31, 2025 (122 days). State A table = $500, State B table = $400.

- State A: $500 x 243/365 = $333
- State B: $400 x 122/365 = $134
- Total = $467

Enter $467 on line 1 of single worksheet if no localities imposed local tax. Otherwise, complete separate State A and State B worksheets entering $333 and $134 respectively.

###### Line 2

If "No," enter -0- on line 2, go to line 3. If "Yes" and lived in same locality all 2025, enter applicable amount based on 2025 income/family size from 2025 Optional Local Sales Tax Tables for your locality. Read down "At least-But less than" columns finding line including your 2025 income. Family size = dependents listed on Form 1040/1040-SR page 1 (plus continuation sheets) plus you and spouse (if joint return). If married not filing jointly, include spouse only under certain circumstances (Publication 501).

###### What if you lived in more than one locality?

Look up table amount for each locality using stated rules. (No table = zero amount.) Multiply each locality's table amount by fraction: numerator = days lived in locality during 2025, denominator = 365. If living in multiple localities with same local tax rate, enter total prorated amounts on line 2. Otherwise, complete separate worksheet lines 2-6 for each locality, entering each prorated amount on applicable worksheet line 2.

**Example.** Lived in Locality 1 January 1-August 31, 2025 (243 days), Locality 2 September 1-December 31, 2025 (122 days). Locality 1 table = $100, Locality 2 table = $150.

- Locality 1: $100 x 243/365 = $67
- Locality 2: $150 x 122/365 = $50
- Total = $117

This amount may not equal your deduction figured on line 6.

###### Line 3

If living in California, check "No" if combined state/local rate = 7.2500%. Otherwise check "Yes," including only rate portion exceeding 7.2500%.

If living in Nevada, check "No" if combined rate = 6.8500%. Otherwise check "Yes," including only rate portion exceeding 6.8500%.

###### What if your local general sales tax rate changed during 2025?

Multiply each rate by fraction: numerator = days rate was effective during 2025, denominator = 365. Enter total prorated rates on line 3.

**Example.** Locality 1: 1% from January 1-September 30, 2025 (273 days); increased to 1.75% October 1-December 31, 2025 (92 days).

- January 1-September 30: 1.00 x 273/365 = 0.748
- October 1-December 31: 1.75 x 92/365 = 0.441
- Total = 1.189

Enter 1.189 on line 3.

###### What if you lived in more than one locality in the same state during 2025?

Complete separate worksheet lines 2-6 for each state locality if living in multiple localities during 2025 with different local tax rates.

For localities without using 2025 Optional Local Sales Tax Tables, multiply local tax rate by fraction: numerator = days lived in locality during 2025, denominator = 365.

**Example.** Lived in Locality 1 January 1-August 31, 2025 (243 days), Locality 2 September 1-December 31, 2025 (122 days). Locality 1 rate = 1%, Locality 2 rate = 1.75%.

- Locality 1: 1.00 x 243/365 = 0.666
- Locality 2: 1.75 x 122/365 = 0.585

Enter 0.666 on Locality 1 worksheet line 3, 0.585 on Locality 2 worksheet line 3.

###### Line 6

If living in multiple localities in same state during 2025, complete line 1 only on first worksheet for that state; complete separate worksheets lines 2-6 for other state localities. If checking "Yes" on line 6 of those worksheets, multiply line 5 by line 1 amount entered for that state on first worksheet.

###### Line 7

Enter state/local general sales taxes paid on specified items:

1. Motor vehicle (car, motorcycle, motor home, RV, SUV, truck, van, off-road vehicle) including leased vehicles. If state sales tax rate exceeds general rate, include only general rate equivalent.
2. Aircraft or boat only if tax rate equaled general rate.
3. Home (mobile home, prefabricated home) or substantial addition/major renovation, only if tax rate equaled general rate AND any apply:
   - State/locality imposes general sales tax on home sales or substantial addition/renovation costs.
   - Purchased materials building home/substantial addition/renovation, paying sales tax directly.
   - Under state law, contractor is your agent in construction/renovation. Contract must authorize contractor acting in your name, following your construction decisions. You're considered to have purchased taxable items and paid sales tax directly.

Exclude trade/business items from sales tax deduction. If receiving 2025 general sales tax refunds, see Refund of general sales taxes earlier.

#### Line 5b - State and Local Real Estate Taxes

Enter state/local real estate taxes paid on non-business real estate only if:

- Taxes assessed uniformly at like rate on all community property.
- Proceeds used for general community/governmental purposes.

Don't include:

- Foreign real estate taxes.
- Itemized service charges to specific property/persons (trash collection, water consumption fees, lawn mowing charges).
- Improvement charges increasing property value (sidewalk assessments). Property improvement costs add to property basis. Deductible if maintaining existing public facility (existing sidewalk repair, interest included).

If mortgage payments include real estate taxes, deduct only amounts actually paid to taxing authority in 2025.

If selling home in 2025, taxes charged to buyer on settlement statement or Form 1099-S box 6 = tax refund. Real estate taxes paid at closing = shown on settlement statement.

**Caution.** Check real estate tax bill for nondeductible itemized charges. If taxing authority or lender doesn't furnish copy, request it.

##### Prepayment of Next Year's Property Taxes

Only 2025 taxes assessed prior to 2026 are deductible for 2025. State/local law determines assessment timing (generally when taxpayer becomes liable).

##### Refunds and Rebates

If receiving 2025 refund/rebate of 2025 real estate taxes, reduce deduction by refund/rebate amount. If receiving 2025 refund/rebate of prior-year taxes, don't reduce 2025 deduction. Include refund/rebate on Schedule 1, line 8z if prior deduction reduced tax (see Recoveries in Publication 525).

#### Line 5c - State and Local Personal Property Taxes

Enter state/local personal property taxes paid, only if based on value alone and imposed yearly.

**Example.** Yearly car registration fee partly value-based, partly weight-based. Deduct only the value-based portion.

##### Prepayment of Next Year's Property Taxes

Only 2025 taxes assessed prior to 2026 are deductible for 2025. State/local law determines assessment timing.

#### Line 5e - State and Local Tax Deduction

If Form 1040/1040-SR line 11b is $500,000 or less ($250,000 or less if married filing separately) AND you didn't complete Form 2555, Form 4563, or exclude Puerto Rico income, enter the smaller of line 5d or $40,000 ($20,000 if married filing separately).

If Form 1040/1040-SR line 11b is more than $500,000 (more than $250,000 if married filing separately) OR you completed Form 2555, Form 4563, or excluded Puerto Rico income, complete the State and Local Tax Deduction Worksheet.

##### State and Local Tax Deduction Worksheet

**If line 5d is $10,000 or less ($5,000 or less if married filing separately),** enter the line 5d amount on line 5e. No further worksheet completion needed.

#### Line 6 - Other Taxes

Enter only one total but list each tax type and amount. Include foreign country income taxes paid and generation-skipping tax (GST) on certain income distributions.

**Tip.** Consider taking credit for foreign taxes instead of deduction. See Schedule 3 instructions, line 1.

Don't include:

- U.S. territory taxes (include on appropriate state/local tax line instead).
- Federal estate tax on income in respect of decedent (include on line 16 instead).
