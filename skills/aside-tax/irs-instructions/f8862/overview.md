## Instructions for Form 8862 - Information To Claim Certain Credits After Disallowance

### Future Developments

For the latest information about developments related to Form 8862 and its instructions, such as legislation enacted after they were published, go to IRS.gov/Form8862.

### What's New

**Social security number (SSN) required to claim the child tax credit (CTC) and additional child tax credit (ACTC).** Starting in tax year 2025, you must have a valid SSN to claim the CTC or ACTC. On joint returns, only one spouse requires a valid SSN. The other spouse must have either an SSN or an individual taxpayer identification number (ITIN).

### Reminders

**Age requirements for taxpayers claiming the earned income credit (EIC) without a qualifying child.** Filers without a qualifying child must be at least age 25 but under age 65 at year-end.

**Qualifying child age requirement for the child tax credit (CTC) and additional child tax credit (ACTC).** A qualifying child for CTC and ACTC purposes must be under age 17 at year-end.

**How to appeal the disallowance period.** If you want to appeal the 2- or 10-year disallowance period preventing you from taking the EIC, CTC, ACTC, ODC, or AOTC, documentation proving entitlement or showing the claim was not due to recklessness, intentional disregard, or fraud is required.

**Delayed refund for returns claiming certain credits.** The IRS cannot issue refunds before mid-February for returns claiming the EIC or the ACTC. This applies to the entire refund, not just the portion associated with these credits.

**Earned income credit (EIC).** If you have not been issued a valid SSN by the return due date (including extensions), you cannot claim the EIC on original or amended returns. An SSN is invalid for EIC purposes if issued solely for federal benefit applications and does not authorize work. Your qualifying child must also have a valid SSN issued by the due date to be counted.

**Child tax credit (CTC) or additional child tax credit (ACTC).** Beginning in 2025, you must have a valid SSN (valid for employment, issued before the due date including extensions) to claim CTC or ACTC. On joint returns, only one spouse needs a valid SSN; the other must have an SSN or ITIN issued by the due date. Your child must have a valid SSN for employment issued before the due date. Children identified by ITIN or adoption taxpayer identification number (ATIN) are not qualifying children for CTC or ACTC.

**Credit for other dependents (ODC).** You (and your spouse on joint returns) must have an SSN or ITIN issued by the return due date (including extensions) to claim the ODC. Dependents used for the ODC must have an SSN, ITIN, or ATIN issued by the due date.

**American opportunity tax credit (AOTC).** You must have an SSN or ITIN issued by the return due date (including extensions) to claim the AOTC. Students must have an SSN, ITIN, or ATIN issued by the due date.

**Note:** If you apply for an ITIN or ATIN by the 2025 return due date (including extensions) and the IRS issues it as a result of your application, the IRS considers it issued by the due date. This applies whether you are applying for yourself or a dependent.
