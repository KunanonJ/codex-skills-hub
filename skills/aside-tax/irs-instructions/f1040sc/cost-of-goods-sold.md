### Part III. Cost of Goods Sold

**Exception for small business taxpayers.** A small business taxpayer is not required to use the capitalization rules for inventory or apply the uniform capitalization rules to the production or acquisition of property.

**Small business taxpayer.** A small business taxpayer is one whose average annual gross receipts do not exceed $5 million for the three preceding tax years (or for the years the business has been in existence if less than three years). If you are a small business taxpayer, you can treat inventoriable items the same as nonincidental materials and supplies (meaning you can deduct them as a current business expense when purchased, rather than when used).

**Treating inventory as nonincidental material or supplies.** If you are a small business taxpayer, you can choose to treat all inventoriable items in the same manner as nonincidental materials and supplies. Under this method, you deduct the cost of inventoriable items in the year purchased, provided this method clearly reflects income. This election is made by filing your tax return using the method. To change from this method to another method, you must file Form 3115. There is no automatic approval for changing from this method.

**Financial accounting treatment of inventories.** You should generally use the same inventory method for federal tax purposes as you use in keeping your books and financial records. If you do not keep books and records, you should use a method that clearly reflects income.

**More information.** See Pub. 538 for more details on inventory methods and cost of goods sold.

**Changing your method of accounting for inventory.** To change your method of accounting for inventory, you must file Form 3115. You must also make a section 481(a) adjustment.

#### Line 33

**Inventory at beginning of year.** If this is your first year in business, enter zero.

#### Line 35

**Inventory at end of year.** If you are using the small business taxpayer exception to treat inventoriable items as nonincidental materials and supplies, you do not need to value your inventory at year-end. In this case, enter zero on line 35.
