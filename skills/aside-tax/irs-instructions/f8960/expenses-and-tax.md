### Part II -- Investment Expenses Allocable to Investment Income

#### Line 9a -- Investment Interest Expense

Enter investment interest expense that is properly allocable to investment income. This includes interest paid on debt incurred to purchase or carry investment property. Generally, this expense is limited under section 163(d) of the Internal Revenue Code.

#### Line 9b -- State, Local, and Foreign Income Tax

Enter state, local, and foreign income taxes that are properly allocable to investment income. This must be limited to taxes deducted in calculating your adjusted gross income (AGI) that relate specifically to investment items.

Note: The $10,000 cap on state and local tax deductions under section 164 applies for regular income tax purposes. For NIIT purposes, state and local income taxes properly allocable to net investment income may be deducted.

#### Line 9c -- Miscellaneous Investment Expenses

Enter miscellaneous expenses that are properly allocable to investment income. These may include:
- Investment advisory fees
- Custodial fees
- Similar expenses directly connected to producing investment income

Subject to applicable limitations.

#### Line 9d -- Total Investment Expenses

Add lines 9a, 9b, and 9c. Enter the total.

### Part III -- Tax Computation

#### Section A -- Individuals

##### Line 10 -- Additional Modifications

Include deductions subject to adjusted gross income limitations under sections 67 or 68, such as miscellaneous itemized deductions and other AGI-limited items. Special rules apply for traders in financial instruments.

##### Line 11 -- Total Deductions

Add lines 9d and 10. Enter the total.

##### Line 12 -- Net Investment Income

Subtract line 11 from line 8. If zero or less, enter -0-.

##### Line 13 -- Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI)

Calculate your MAGI for net investment income tax purposes.

**Individuals.** Generally, your MAGI is the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11 (your AGI), increased by:
- The amount excluded from income as foreign earned income under section 911(a)(1), net of the deductions and exclusions disallowed with respect to the foreign earned income.

If you did not file Form 2555, your MAGI is the same as your AGI. Enter the amount from Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11.

If you filed Form 2555, use the MAGI Worksheet to compute your MAGI.

##### Line 14 -- Threshold Amount

Enter the applicable threshold based on your filing status:

| Filing Status | Threshold Amount |
|---|---|
| Married Filing Jointly or Qualifying Surviving Spouse | $250,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $125,000 |
| Single or Head of Household | $200,000 |

##### Line 15 -- Excess of MAGI Over Threshold

Subtract line 14 from line 13. If zero or less, enter -0-.

##### Line 16 -- Smaller of Line 12 or Line 15

Enter the smaller of line 12 (net investment income) or line 15 (excess of MAGI over threshold).

##### Line 17 -- Net Investment Income Tax

Multiply line 16 by 3.8% (0.038). Enter the result.

Enter this amount on Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 12.

#### Section B -- Estates and Trusts

##### Line 18a -- Net Investment Income (From Line 12)

Enter the amount from line 12 for the estate or trust.

##### Line 18b -- Deductions for Distributions of Net Investment Income and Charitable Contributions

Estates and trusts may deduct:
- Distributions of net investment income to beneficiaries
- Charitable contributions (subject to section 170(b) for certain trusts)

Line 18b of Form 8960 was updated to reflect changes outlined in P.L. 115-97, section 13542, that amended the way the S portion of an ESBT accounts for charitable contribution deductions under section 170(b) instead of section 641(c).

##### Line 18c -- Undistributed Net Investment Income

Subtract line 18b from line 18a. This represents net investment income that the estate or trust retains rather than distributes to beneficiaries during the tax year.

##### Line 19a -- Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)

For estates and trusts, AGI is calculated as defined in section 67(e).

For ESBTs with both S and non-S portions, the ESBT determines its AGI, solely for purposes of NIIT, by adding the net income or net loss from the S portion to the AGI of the non-S portion.

##### Line 19b -- Highest Tax Bracket Threshold

Enter the dollar amount at which the highest tax bracket in section 1(e) begins for the tax year.

##### Line 19c -- Excess of AGI Over Threshold

Subtract line 19b from line 19a. If zero or less, enter -0-.

##### Line 20 -- Smaller of Line 18c or Line 19c

Enter the smaller of undistributed net investment income (line 18c) or excess AGI over the threshold (line 19c).

##### Line 21 -- Net Investment Income Tax

Multiply line 20 by 3.8% (0.038). Enter the result.

### Special Rules for Estates and Trusts

#### Qualified Funeral Trusts (QFTs)

The NIIT applies to the QFT (as defined in section 685) by treating each beneficiary's interest in that beneficiary's contract as a separate trust. When multiple beneficiary contracts exceed threshold amounts, the QFT must:
- Complete consolidated Form 8960 using only beneficiary contracts with net investment income exceeding the threshold
- Enter the number of qualifying contracts on line 19b
- Multiply contract count by the annual threshold amount

**Example.** A 2024 QFT with two beneficiary contracts ($16,000 interest and $21,000 dividends, no deductions) with $15,200 threshold results in $250.80 NIIT liability.

#### Electing Small Business Trusts (ESBTs)

ESBTs compute NIIT through a three-step process:

1. The ESBT separately calculates the undistributed net investment income of the S portion and non-S portion according to the general rules for trusts under chapter 1 of the Code, and then combines the undistributed net investment income.

2. The ESBT determines AGI by adding S portion net income/loss to non-S portion AGI.

3. Combined undistributed net investment income is compared against excess AGI over section 1(e) threshold.

For ESBTs with an S portion and a non-S portion, use Form 8960 as a worksheet for calculating the amounts to enter on line 7 and line 19a.

### Recordkeeping Requirements

Keep all records throughout the investment's life showing:
- Basis calculations
- Investment disposition details
- Passive activity determinations
- CFC/PFIC ownership documentation
- Election statements and certifications
- Prior-year treatment, particularly for items subject to carryback or carryforward treatment
