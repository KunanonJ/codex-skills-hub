### General Instructions

#### Purpose of Form

Use Form 8960 to calculate the Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) for individuals, estates, and trusts. The NIIT applies at 3.8% to the lesser of net investment income or the amount by which modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) exceeds applicable thresholds.

#### Who Must File

Attach Form 8960 to your return when your MAGI exceeds the applicable threshold amount and you have net investment income.

**Threshold Amounts by Filing Status:**

| Filing Status | Threshold Amount |
|---|---|
| Married Filing Jointly or Qualifying Surviving Spouse | $250,000 |
| Married Filing Separately | $125,000 |
| Single or Head of Household | $200,000 |

**Nonresident Aliens.** The NIIT does not apply to nonresident alien (NRA) individuals.

**Dual-Status Individuals.** If you were a dual-status individual, you are subject to the NIIT only for the portion of the year you were a U.S. resident.

**Section 6013 Elections.** If you and your spouse elect to file a joint return under section 6013(g) or 6013(h), you can also elect to apply the joint return election for NIIT purposes.

### Definitions

#### Net Investment Income

Generally, net investment income includes gross income from interest, dividends, annuities, royalties, and rents, unless they are derived from the ordinary course of a trade or business that is not (a) a passive activity, or (b) a trade or business of trading in financial instruments or commodities.

Net investment income also includes:
- Income from passive activities and trading businesses
- Net gains from property dispositions (with exceptions)

Net investment income is reduced by properly allocable deductions.

**Excluded items** include:
- Wages and self-employment income
- Unemployment benefits
- Tax-exempt interest
- Qualified retirement plan distributions (sections 401, 403(a), 403(b), 408, 408A, 457(b))
- Social Security benefits
- Alaska Permanent Fund Dividends

#### Section 1411 Trade or Business

Generally, a trade or business that is either a passive activity for the taxpayer or is a trade or business of trading in financial instruments or commodities.

#### Properly Allocable Deductions

Deductions allowed against income tax that are properly allocable to net investment income items. You may use any reasonable method to allocate investment expenses between investment income and other income. The regulations allow flexibility in determining what constitutes a "reasonable method" for this allocation.

Items not deductible in calculating net investment income include:
- Expenses allocable to excluded income
- Expenses related to tax-exempt interest
- Deductions subject to specific limitations under the tax code

#### Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI)

For individuals, MAGI is your adjusted gross income (AGI) increased by the amount excluded from income as foreign earned income under section 911(a)(1), net of the deductions and exclusions disallowed with respect to the foreign earned income.

#### Regrouping

Generally, you may not regroup activities unless your grouping was clearly inappropriate when originally made, or has become clearly inappropriate because of changed facts and circumstances. However, under the NIIT "fresh start" election, you may regroup for the first tax year you are subject to the NIIT (without the effect of the regrouping).

#### Real Estate Professionals

If you materially participate in rental real estate activities for more than 500 hours during the current year (or any five of the preceding ten years), rental income from those activities is excluded from NIIT treatment.

#### CFC and PFIC Elections

A "section 1.1411-10(g) election" allows treating CFC and QEF inclusions consistently between regular income tax and NIIT purposes. This election must be made by the first tax year you are subject to NIIT and include CFCs/QEFs by name and identification number on an attached statement.

#### Net Operating Losses (Section 1411 NOLs)

Section 1411 NOLs are calculated using only items affecting net investment income. The "applicable portion" represents the percentage of regular income tax NOL that qualifies as a section 1411 NOL for carryforward purposes.

When applying carryback/carryforward NOLs, deduct the "applicable portion" equal to the percentage of regular income tax NOL representing section 1411 NOL. Calculation requires separate Form 172 showing only section 1411 items.
