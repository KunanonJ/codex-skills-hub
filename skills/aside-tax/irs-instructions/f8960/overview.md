## Instructions for Form 8960 - Net Investment Income Tax (Individuals, Estates, and Trusts)

### Future Developments

For the latest information about developments related to Form 8960 and its instructions, such as legislation enacted after they were published, go to IRS.gov/Form8960.

### What's New

- Digital assets may be subject to tax under section 1411(a).
- ESBTs and charitable contribution deduction changes under P.L. 115-97.
- Schedule C and Schedule E income may be subject to NIIT.

### Reminders

- Digital assets. Digital asset transactions may be subject to NIIT.
- ESBTs. Line 18b of Form 8960 was updated to reflect changes outlined in P.L. 115-97, section 13542, that amended the way the S portion of an ESBT accounts for charitable contribution deductions under section 170(b) instead of section 641(c).
