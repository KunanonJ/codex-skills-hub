### Specific Instructions

### Part I -- Investment Income

#### Line 1 -- Taxable Interest

Enter taxable interest from Form 1040/1040-SR line 2b, Form 1041 line 1, or Form 1041-QFT line 1a.

Exclude interest from non-section 1411 trade/business activities using line 7 adjustment. Self-charged interest from nonpassive partnerships/S corporations may require line 7 adjustment.

#### Line 2 -- Ordinary Dividends

Enter ordinary dividends from Form 1040/1040-SR line 3b, Form 1041 line 2a, or Form 1041-QFT line 2a.

Exclude employer ESOP dividends under section 404(k) and Alaska Permanent Fund Dividends via line 7 negative modification.

#### Line 3 -- Annuities

Enter gross annuity income excluding amounts from qualified retirement plans (sections 401, 403(a), 403(b), 408, 408A, 457(b)).

Report annuity distributions marked with code "D" in Form 1099-R box 7 using the taxable amount from box 2a.

#### Line 4a -- Rental, Royalty, Partnership, S Corporation, Trust, etc.

Report amounts from Schedule 1 (Form 1040) lines 3 and 5, Form 1041 lines 3 and 5, Form 1041-QFT line 4 portion, or Form 1040-NR adjusted for U.S. residency period. Includes both passive and nonpassive income combined.

#### Line 4b -- Adjustment for Net Income or Loss Derived in the Ordinary Course of a Non-Section 1411 Trade or Business

Enter gains as negative numbers and losses as positive numbers for excluded items:
- Net income/loss from nonpassive section 162 trades/businesses
- Nonpassive rental income from real estate professionals meeting safe harbor requirements
- Rental income grouped with nonpassive trades/businesses
- Suspended passive losses allowed under section 469(f)(1)(A)
- Other nonpassive rental income from partnerships/S corporations properly reported on Schedule K-1 line 3

#### Line 4c -- Net Passive Income/Loss

Calculated automatically (line 4a plus line 4b). Represents passive activity income/loss subject to NIIT.

#### Lines 5a through 5d -- Net Gain or Loss From Disposition of Property

##### Line 5a -- Net Gain or Loss

Combine capital gains/losses and ordinary gains/losses from:
- Form 1040/1040-SR lines 7 and Schedule 1 line 4
- Form 1041 lines 4 and 7
- Form 1041-QFT line 3 and applicable portion of line 4
- Form 1040-NR statements reflecting U.S. residency periods

Excludes gains from principal residence sales (section 121) and like-kind exchanges (section 1031).

##### Line 5b -- Net Gain or Loss From Disposition of Property Excluded From Net Investment Income

Enter excluded gains as negative numbers and excluded losses as positive numbers. Adjustments include:
- Gains/losses from non-section 1411 trade/business property dispositions
- Net unrealized appreciation (NUA) in employer securities from qualified plans
- Capital loss carryforward adjustments for excluded losses
- Long-term capital gain amounts from QEFs without section 1.1411-10(g) elections under section 1293(a)(1)(B)

**Special rule for substantially appreciated property.** Fair market value exceeding 120% of adjusted basis may be recharacterized as nonpassive gain, except when property was passive for 20% of holding period or entire 2-year pre-disposition period.

**Capital loss carryforwards.** Require annual adjustment when excluded capital losses exceed excluded capital gains. The adjustment equals the lesser of prior-year carryforward amounts or current-year excluded losses exceeding excluded gains.

##### Line 5c -- Adjustment From Disposition of Partnership Interest or S Corporation Stock

Enter gain or loss adjustment calculated using worksheet line 3(d). Materially participating individuals disposing of partnership interests or S corporation stock in nonpassive, non-trading trades/businesses must calculate adjustments. Attach required statement listing:
- Partnership/S corporation name and taxpayer ID
- Disposition gain/loss amounts for regular income tax
- Information provided by entity
- Adjustments for basis differences from CFC/QEF ownership

For installment sales/private annuities, calculate adjustment in disposition year even if prior to 2013. Attach statement in initial NIIT-filing year; thereafter attach statement noting "Adjustment relates to a deferred recognition sale first reported on line 5c of the [year] return."

##### Line 5d -- Total Net Gain or Loss

Sum of lines 5a, 5b, and 5c combined. Represents total net disposition gains and losses included in NIIT calculation.

#### Lines 5a-5d -- Net Gains and Losses Worksheet

**Section 1: Beginning Net Gains and Losses**

1. Enter total of capital gains/losses from Form 1040 or 1040-SR line 7; Form 1041 line 4; Form 1041-QFT line 3; or Form 1040-NR statement reflecting U.S. residency portion. Transfer this amount to line 5a.

**Section 2: Gains and Losses Excluded from Net Investment Income**

2(a). Net gains from disposition of property in non-section 1411 trade/business (enter as negative)

2(b). Net losses from disposition of property in non-section 1411 trade/business (enter as positive)

2(c). Net losses from former passive activity allowed under section 469(f)(1)(A)

2(d). Gains recognized in current year from installment sale/private annuity for non-section 1411 property

2(e). Net gain attributable to NUA in employer securities

2(f). For QEFs without section 1.1411-10(g) election, amount treated as long-term capital gain under section 1293(a)(1)(B)

2(g). Other gains/losses included or excluded from net investment income

2(h). Amount from line 2(i) of prior year return (enter as positive)

2(i). Capital loss carryover adjustment (enter as negative) -- lesser of 2(i)(1) or 2(i)(2)

2(j). Sum of lines 2(a) through 2(i). Transfer to line 5b.

**Section 3: Adjustment for Partnership Interest or S Corporation Stock Dispositions**

3(a)(i). Net gain from disposition of partnership/S corporation included on line 5a

3(a)(ii). Net gain included in net investment income after Regulations section 1.1411-7

3(a)(iii). Difference between 3(a)(i) and 3(a)(ii)

3(b)(i). Net loss from disposition of partnership/S corporation included on line 5a

3(b)(ii). Net loss included in net investment income after Regulations section 1.1411-7

3(b)(iii). Difference between 3(b)(i) and 3(b)(ii)

3(c)(i). Gain recognized in current year from prior installment sale/private annuity on partnership/S corporation

3(c)(ii). Adjustment attributable to such gain

3(c)(iii). Subtract 3(c)(ii) from 3(c)(i)

3(d). Combine amounts on lines 3(a)(iii), 3(b)(iii), and 3(c)(iii). Transfer to line 5c.

**Section 4: Summary**

4. Sum of items on lines 5a-5c (add lines 1, 2(j), and 3(d)). Transfer to line 5d.

#### Line 6 -- Adjustments to Gain or Loss From Disposition of Interest in Partnership or S Corporation (CFCs and PFICs)

Report adjustments for controlled foreign corporations and passive foreign investment companies not held in section 1411 trades/businesses or marked-to-market under chapter 1 provisions.

- Section 1296 mark-to-market PFIC rules require including income increases under section 1296(a)(1) and deducting decreases under section 1296(a)(2).
- Section 1291 PFIC rules require including excess distributions treated as dividends and gains treated as excess distributions.
- CFC/QEF section 1.1411-10(g) election holders include section 951(a), 951A, or 1293(a) inclusions derived from the corporation. Section 1293(a)(1)(B) inclusions may appear elsewhere on Form 8960.
- CFC/QEF shareholders without elections must include distributions of previously taxed income not subject to regular income tax. Special basis calculation rules and investment interest expense adjustments may apply.
- Shareholders holding CFCs/QEFs since 2013 and making elections after 2013 face special rules regarding distributions of previously taxed income.

#### Line 7 -- Other Modifications to Investment Income

Report modifications not specified on lines 1-6. Include:
- Section 1411 NOL deductions (entered as negative amounts)
- Section 62(a)(1) deductions allocable to passive activities/trading businesses
- Estate/trust distributions
- Section 404(k) dividends (negative modification)
- Self-charged interest adjustments
- Deduction recoveries
- Form 8814 parent election amounts
- Substitute interest/dividend payments
- Notional principal contract net payments
- Gain/loss from annuity/life insurance contract dispositions
- Alaska Permanent Fund Dividends (negative modification)

**Section 1411 NOL.** Calculation requires determining the lesser of: (1) NOL computed using only net investment income items and properly allocable deductions on Form 8960, or (2) regular income tax NOL. When applying carryback/carryforward NOLs, deduct the "applicable portion" equal to the percentage of regular income tax NOL representing section 1411 NOL.

#### Line 8 -- Net Investment Income

Calculated as sum of lines 1, 2, 3, 4c, 5d, 6, and 7. If zero or less, enter -0-.
